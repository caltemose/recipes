Walnut and Red Pepper Spread

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.75 cup(s) walnuts toasted and skinned
  • 2 tablespoon(s) bread crumbs fresh, toasted
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 1.25 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) roasted and peeled
  • 6 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) roasted, seeded, and peeled
  • 2 tablespoon(s) pomegranate molasses
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon juice

Directions

  1. In a blender or food processor, grind the walnuts, bread crumbs, and cumin together. Add the peppers, chilies, and pomegranate molasses. With the machine running, drizzle in the olive oil until the mixture becomes creamy. Add the red pepper flakes, salt, and lemon juice. Serve at once or, for the best flavor, cover and refrigerate for 4-5 days.
