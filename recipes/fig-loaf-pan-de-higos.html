Fig Loaf (Pan de Higos)

Savoring Spain and Portugal

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound(s) dried figs Mission figs, stems removed and finely chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) almonds toasted and finely chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) hazelnuts toasted and finely chopped
  • 0.25 cup(s) powdered sugar plus more for sprinkling foil
  • 1 teaspoon(s) ground cinnamon
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground cloves
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon zest
  • 2 tablespoon(s) brandy or anise liqueur
  • 2 ounce(s) semi-sweet chocolate melted

Directions

  1. In a food processor, combine the figs, almonds, hazelnuts, 1/2 C powered sugar, cinnamon, cloves and lemon zest. Pulse until well combined. Stir in the liqueur or brandy and chocolate to moisten the mixture, which will be very stiff.
  2. Sprinkle a large square of heavy-duty aluminum foil with powdered sugar. Shape the fig mixture into a large loaf and wrap it in the foil. Refrigerate for at least 2 days or as long as 3 months.
  3. To serve, dip a very sharp knife in hot water and cut the loaf into slices.
