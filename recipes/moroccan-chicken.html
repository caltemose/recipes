Moroccan Chicken

CIA

Ingredients

  • 8 unit(s) chicken legs skinned
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 0.5 cup(s) kalamata olive(s) chopped
  • 6 unit(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and chopped
  • 5 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) roasted and chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) cilantro chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) parsley chopped
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) cayenne pepper
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) paprika
  • 1 teaspoon(s) garlic powder
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground cinnamon
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil

Directions

  1. To make the spice paste: In a large, shallow bowl, combine all the paste ingredients. Add the chicken legs and turn them to coat them evenly with the paste.
  2. In a large cast-iron skillet or flameproof casserole over medium heat, heat the olive oil and brown the chicken legs on all sides. Transfer the chicken to a plate. Add the broth to the pan and stir over medium heat to dissolve all the browned bits on the bottom of the pan.
  3. Add the chicken legs and olives to the pan. Bring the broth to a simmer, cover, and braise over low heat until the chicken is fork tender, about 1 hour. Remove the chicken legs from the cooking liquid and let cool until they can be handled.
  4. Pull the meat from the bones and put it in a large bowl. Add the tomatoes, roasted peppers, fresh herbs, and cooking liquid. Toss the mixture well and season generously with salt and pepper. Cover and refrigerate for 1 or 2 hours, or until thoroughly chilled.
  5. To serve, scoop the chicken mixture into fresh pita pockets.
