Potato Noodles with Grilled Vegetables

CIA

Ingredients

  • 5 unit(s) potato scrubbed
  • 0.5 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.12 teaspoon(s) nutmeg grated
  • 0.12 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 0.5 cup(s) olive oil
  • 2 tablespoon(s) Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon(s) thyme minced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) salt
  • 1 tablespoon(s) garlic roasted
  • 1 tablespoon(s) ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon(s) tarragon minced
  • 1 teaspoon(s) parsley minced
  • 2 unit(s) red onion(s) quartered
  • 2 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) halved and seeded
  • 2 unit(s) yellow bell peppers halved and seeded
  • 2 unit(s) portobello mushroom(s) stemmed
  • 3 unit(s) zucchini cut lengthwise into slices
  • 2 unit(s) yellow squash cut lengthwise into slices
  • 4 unit(s) tomato(es) plum tomatoes, halved and seeded
  • 0.75 cup(s) artichoke hearts drained canned
  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil

Directions

  1. To make the noodles: Cook the potatoes in boiling salted water until tender. Drain. While the potatoes are still very hot, coarsely shred them into a bowl using a box grater. Immediately add the flour, eggs, salt, nutmeg, and pepper. Stir until evenly blended.
  2. Dredge your hands in flour. Break off a grape-sized bit of the potato dough. Roll the dough out to make a 3-inch-long noodle. Place on a lightly floured surface. Repeat until all the dough is rolled.
  3. Bring a large pot of salted water to a simmer. Roll the noodles into the water and cook for about 4 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the noodles to a colander. Drain, cover, and refrigerate.
  4. Light a fire in a charcoal grill. Twenty minutes before grilling, combine all the ingredients for the marinade in a large bowl. Add the vegetables and toss until they are evenly coated.
  5. Let the vegetables marinate for 20 minutes. Drain, reserving the marinade. Grill the onions over a medium-hot fire until they are fully cooked and tender. Grill the peppers, skin-side down, until the skin blisters. Remove them from the grill and scrape away the charred skin. Grill the remaining vegetables until they are grill-marked and wilted. Set all the vegetables aside and keep warm.
  6. In a large saucepan or skillet over medium heat, heat the oil and brown the potato noodles carefully on both sides. Arrange the noodles in a warmed bowl or platter and surround them with the warm grilled vegetables. Drizzle them with some of the reserved marinade.
