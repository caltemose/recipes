Avocado, Tomato, and Corn Salad with Aged Cheddar and Chipotle-Sherry Vinaigrette

CIA

Ingredients

  • 4 unit(s) corn corn ears, shucked
  • 1 head(s) frisee small, cored
  • 8 ounce(s) salad greens baby greens
  • 1.5 cup(s) chipotle-sherry vinaigrette #2
  • 4 unit(s) tomato(es) beefsteak, cut into 1/4-inch-thick slices
  • 4 unit(s) avocado peeled, pitted, and halved
  • 2 pound(s) cherry tomato(es) cut in half
  • 2 unit(s) red onion(s) cut into 1/8--inch-thick slices
  • 8 ounce(s) cheddar aged cheese, crumbled into bite-sized pieces

Directions

  1. In a large pot of salted boiling water, cook the corn until tender, about 10 minutes. Drain, rinse under cold water, and let drain again. Cut the kernels from the cob, but don't go too deeply into the cob. Use the back of a knife or a spoon to scrape the corn and milk from the cobs. Set aside.
  2. Toss the frisee and baby greens with all but 2 tablespoons of the vinaigrette. Arrange the greens on a chilled platter or individual salad plates. Place the sliced tomatoes on the greens. Cut each avocado half into a thinly sliced fan and place on top of the tomatoes. Scatter the small tomatoes, corn, red onions, and cheddar cheese over the salad. Drizzle the remaining vinaigrette over the salads and serve at once.
