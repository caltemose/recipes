Mango Lemonade

The Indian Vegan Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) water
  • 0.33 cup(s) lime juice
  • 0.33 cup(s) sugar
  • 1 cup(s) mango nectar
  • 1 sprig(s) mint for garnish

Directions

  1. Combine water, lemon juice, and sugar. Add mango nectar.
  2. Serve over crushed ice. Garnish with mint leaves.
