Strawberry-Thyme Prosecco

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) strawberries fresh or frozen, muddled (or strawberry nectar)
  • 2 teaspoon(s) thyme minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice or lemon juice
  • 1 bottle(s) prosecco

Directions

  1. Mix and serve chilled or over ice.
