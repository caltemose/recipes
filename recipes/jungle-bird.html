Jungle Bird

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 1 cup(s) ice cubes
  • 1.5 ounce(s) rum
  • 1.5 ounce(s) pineapple juice
  • 0.75 ounce(s) Cocchi rosa Americano
  • 0.5 ounce(s) lime juice
  • 0.5 ounce(s) simple syrup
  • 1 unit(s) ice cubes 2-inch cube
  • 1 to taste nutmeg grated
  • 1 pinch salt

Directions

  1. Combine the ice cubes, rum, pineapple juice, Cocchi, lime juice and simply syrup in a shaker. Cover and shake for 15 to 30 seconds, until chilled and frothy.
  2. Strain the rum mixture into an old-fashioned glass over the 2-inch cube of ice. Garnish with nutmeg and salt. Serve immediately.
