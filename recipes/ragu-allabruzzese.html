Ragu all'Abruzzese

The Glorious Pasta of Italy

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil not EVOO
  • 6 ounce(s) beef chuck roast cut into 4 equal pieces
  • 6 ounce(s) pork shoulder cut into 3 equal pieces
  • 6 ounce(s) lamb shoulder cut into 3 equal pieces
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 3 pound(s) canned tomatoes whole or diced, with their juice
  • 1 unit(s) yellow onion(s) finely chopped

Directions

  1. Warm the vegetable oil in a large Dutch oven or other heavy-bottomed pot placed over medium heat. Season the pieces of meat with salt and pepper and add them to the pot. Brown for 3-4 minutes, then turn the pieces to brown the other side, another 3-4 minutes. Remove the pieces to a deep plate or bowl. Set the pot aside.
  2. Pass the tomatoes through a food mill fitted with the disk with the smallest holes. Discard the solids. Set the milled tomatoes aside.
  3. Return the pot to medium heat and add the extra-virgin olive oil.&nbsp; Stir in the onion, reduce the heat to medium-low, and sautéed for about 5 minutes, or until the onion is shiny and beginning to soften.&nbsp;
  4. Pour in the tomatoes, raise the heat to medium-high, and bring to a simmer.
  5. Return the meat to the pot and reduce the heat to medium-low or low to maintain a gentle simmer.&nbsp; Cover partially and let the sauce simmer, stirring it from time to time, for about 3 hours, or until the meat is very tender and the sauce is thickened.&nbsp;
  6. Add a splash or two of water if the sauce thickens too much before the meat is done. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper, if you like.
  7. Turn off the heat. Remove the meat from the pot before using the sauce. Serve the sauce over pasta alla chittara. Serve the meat as a second course, or save it as a stuffing for cannelloni or ravioli.
  8. The sauce can be stored in the fridge for 3 days or in the freezer for 3 months.
