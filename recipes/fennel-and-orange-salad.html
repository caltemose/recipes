Fennel and Orange Salad

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) fennel bulb(s) trimmed and cored
  • 2 unit(s) orange(s)
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 0.5 cup(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. Thinly slice the fennel with a sharp knife or a mandolin. Cut the skin from the orange and cut the orange into crosswise slices.
  2. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oils, salt, and pepper together to make a vinaigrette. Reserve 2 tablespoons of the parsley for garnish. Put the fennel and remaining parsley in a bowl and toss with 3 tablespoons of the vinaigrette.
  3. Season the orange slices with salt and pepper. Drizzle them with the remaining vinaigrette. Place the fennel and oranges on chilled plates and garnish with the reserved parsley.
