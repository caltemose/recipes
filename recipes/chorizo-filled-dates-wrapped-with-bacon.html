Chorizo-Filled Dates Wrapped with Bacon

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) chorizo about 4 oz
  • 24 unit(s) medjool dates pitted
  • 6 slice(s) bacon cut into quarters

Directions

  1. Remove the casing from the chorizo sausages and cut them into 24 small sticks the length of the dates. Press one piece of chorizo into each date and mold the date around the chorizo.
  2. Wrap each date with a piece of bacon and set it seam-side down on a baking sheet.
  3. When ready to serve, preheat the oven to 375 F. Bake the dates until the bacon is crisp, about 8 to 12 minutes. Drain the dates briefly on paper towels and serve while still very hot.
