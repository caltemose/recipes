Fiery Ginger Ale Concentrate

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 125 gram(s) candied ginger coarsely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s) lightly crushed
  • 575 gram(s) water 20 oz

Directions

  1. Bring all ingredients to a boil over high heat and keep it there for 2 minutes. Then kill the heat and allow to steep for 1 hour.
  2. Strain, cool completely and refrigerate in a sealed container for up to 1 month.
  3. When you desire a glass, simply mix 2 oz syrup with 6-8 oz of cold sparking water.
