Tostada Salad with Tomatillo Salsa

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 1 cup(s) black beans dried
  • 2 can(s) tomatillo(s) drained and chopped
  • 0.33 cup(s) cilantro chopped, lightly packed
  • 0.25 cup(s) red onion(s)
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 0.5 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) seeded and minced
  • 1 cup(s) vegetable oil
  • 6 unit(s) corn tortilla(s)
  • 2 cup(s) monterey jack cheese coarsely shredded
  • 1 head(s) romaine thinly sliced

Directions

  1. Pick over the black beans and discard any misshapen beans or stones. Rinse the beans and drain. Place in a bowl, add plenty of water to cover, and let soak for 3 hours.
  2. Drain the beans and place in a saucepan with water to cover by 2 inches. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to low and simmer, uncovered, until the beans are tender, about 1 hour. Remove from the heat and drain. Set aside.
  3. To make the salsa, in a bowl, stir together the tomatillos, cilantro, onion, lime juice, jalapeño, and salt and pepper to taste. Set aside.
  4. In a frying pan over medium-high heat, warm the vegetable oil. When it is hot, slip a tortilla into the oil and cook until golden and almost crisp, 1-2 minutes. Using tongs, transfer to paper towels to drain. Repeat with the remaining tortillas.
  5. To serve, place each tortilla on a plate, distribute the beans, cheese, lettuces, and salsa evenly over the tortillas.
