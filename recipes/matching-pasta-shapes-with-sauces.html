Matching Pasta Shapes with Sauces

The Glorious Pasta of Italy

Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Tiny pasta shapes, such as stalline (little stars) or acini di pepe (peppercorns), are eaten with a spoon and are best in broth soup.
    2. Medium-size shapes, such as medium shells or ditalini, go well in hearty soups such as pasta and bean soup.
    3. Short, story shapes, such as rigatoni, penne, and tortiglioni or cavatappi, have hollows and ridge that capture sauce and with well with robust, chunky sauces and rich cream sauces.
    4. Thin, long noodles, such as capellini and fedelini, are delicate and therefore pair best with lighter, smooth sauces, such as a light tomato sauce or a butter- or olive oil-based sauce.
    5. Flat noodles, such as fettuccine, marry well with slinky butter and cream sauces.
    6. Long, heftier noodles, such as spaghetti and bucatini, stand up well to rustic sauces, such as pesto and carbonara.
