Beet-Orange Salad

CIA

Ingredients

  • 4 unit(s) beet(s) red, greens trimmed to 1 inch
  • 4 unit(s) beet(s) golden, greens trimmed to 1 inch
  • 2 tablespoon(s) white wine vinegar
  • 2 teaspoon(s) orange zest
  • 0.25 cup(s) orange juice
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 5 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 3 unit(s) orange(s) seedless, peeled and sliced into thin rounds

Directions

  1. Preheat the grill to medium-high. Wrap the beets with foil, place them on the covered grill, and roast them until they are fork-tender, approximately 1 hour, depending on the size.
  2. While the beets are roasting, combine the vinegar, orange zest, orange juice, salt, and cayenne in a medium bowl. While whisking, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until incorporated.
  3. When the beets are cool enough to handle, trim them and slip off the skins using a paring knife. Slice the beets into rounds and add them to the bowl with the dressing while still slightly warm. Toss the beets well to coat with the dressing and let them stand at room temperature for at least 30 minutes. Just before serving, add the oranges and toss well.
