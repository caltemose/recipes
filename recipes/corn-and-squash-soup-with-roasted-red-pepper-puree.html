Corn and Squash Soup with Roasted Red Pepper Puree

CIA

Ingredients

  • 6 cup(s) water
  • 3 sprig(s) basil
  • 1 head(s) garlic halved horizontally
  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 1 cup(s) onion(s) diced
  • 1 cup(s) yellow squash diced
  • 3 cup(s) corn about 6 ears
  • 1 to taste roasted red pepper puree

Directions

  1. In a stockpot, combine the water, basil and garlic. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to a simmer, and cook over medium heat for 30 minutes, skimming the surface if necessary. Strain the broth and set aside.
  2. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat and saute the onions until translucent, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the squash and cook for about 5 minutes. Add the corn, salt, and pepper. Cook over low heat for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the garlic broth and bring the soup to a simmer. Adjust the seasoning with salt if necessary. Puree the soup and strain it through a fine-meshed sieve.
  5. To serve, bring the soup back to a full boil. Ladle it into warmed soup bowls or plates and swirl some of the red pepper puree through each portion.
