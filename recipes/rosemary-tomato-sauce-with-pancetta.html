Rosemary Tomato Sauce with Pancetta

The Glorious Pasta of Italy

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 clove(s) garlic large, lightly crushed
  • 6 ounce(s) pancetta cut into small dice
  • 0.5 unit(s) red onion(s) cut into small dice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) rosemary fresh, minced
  • 2 can(s) canned tomatoes 2-28 oz cans, diced, with their juice

Directions

  1. Put the olive oil and garlic in a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan and place over medium heat. Cook, turning the garlic frequently and using a wooden spoon or silicone spatula to press it into the oil, for about 5 minutes, or until the garlic is fragrant and beings to turn gold. remove and discard the garlic.&nbsp;
  2. Add the pancetta and sauce, stirring frequently, for about 5 minutes or until it being to crisp and brown. Stir in the onion and rosemary and sauce, stirring frequently, for about 7 minutes, or until the onion is wilted.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes and bring the sauce to a boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and let the sauce simmer gently for about 30 minutes or until thickened. Season with salt.
