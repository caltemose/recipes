Barley Water

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 quart(s) water
  • 1 cup(s) barley hulled
  • 2 unit(s) lemon(s)
  • 0.25 cup(s) honey orange blossom

Directions

  1. Combine water and barley in a medium saucepan, cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 30 minutes.
  2. Peel lemons with a vegetable peeler, being careful to avoid the bitter white pith. Juice the lemons into a 3-quart pitcher and add the lemon peel.
  3. Strain the barley water through a fine-mesh strainer into the pitcher. Add the honey and stir to combine. Refrigerate until chilled.
