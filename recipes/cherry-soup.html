Cherry Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 10 ounce(s) cherries fresh or canned
  • 2 cup(s) white wine
  • 1 cup(s) water
  • 2 ounce(s) sugar or to taste
  • 1 unit(s) lemon(s) zest and juice
  • 1.25 cup(s) sour cream or yogurt

Directions

  1. Pit the cherries and reserve the fruit. Add the pits, stems, and any juice to the wine and water in a saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a boil. Simmer, uncovered, for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain the liquid into a clean pan and add the reserved fruit, sugar, and lemon. Bring to a boil then, away from direct heat, whisk in the sour cream. Chill well before serving.
