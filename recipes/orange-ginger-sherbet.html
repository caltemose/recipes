Orange-Ginger Sherbet

The Indian Vegan Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup(s) sugar
  • 6 tablespoon(s) ginger grated
  • 3.5 cup(s) water
  • 6 unit(s) orange(s) or 3 C juice
  • 2 unit(s) lime(s)

Directions

  1. In a small skillet, combine sugar, ginger and 1 C water. Bring to boil, reduce heat and simmer 10 min. Cool completely. Strain.
  2. Squeeze orange and lime juice. Strain.&nbsp;&nbsp;
  3. Combine ginger water, juice, and 2-1/2 C water. Chill.
  4. Garnish with orange and lime rinds.
