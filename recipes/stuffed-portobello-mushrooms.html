Stuffed Portobello Mushrooms

CIA

Ingredients

  • 6 unit(s) portobello mushroom(s) stemmed
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 3 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 8 ounce(s) sausage Italian, casings removed
  • 2 cup(s) bread crumbs dried
  • 1 cup(s) provolone shredded
  • 0.25 cup(s) parsley minced

Directions

  1. Place the mushrooms in a shallow dish and drizzle with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil. Grill or broil the mushroom caps, smooth side facing the fire, for 2 to 3 minutes. Remove and reserve.
  2. Remove the sausage from the casings. Preheat the oven to 425 F. In a medium saucepan or skillet over medium heat, saute the meat, stirring to break it up, until lightly browned. Add the bread crumbs, cheese, and parsley and mix well. Add the remaining tablespoon of olive oil and stir.
  3. Pack the stuffing into the mushroom caps. Place in a baking dish and bake until the stuffing turns golden brown, about 12 to 15 minutes.
