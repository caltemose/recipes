Broccoli Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 1.5 pound(s) broccoli florets
  • 3 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 2 stalk(s) celery 2-3 stalks, finely chopped
  • 2 unit(s) leek(s) white parts only, chopped
  • 4 tablespoon(s) all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 quart(s) chicken broth hot
  • 0.5 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoon(s) chive(s) snipped, to serve

Directions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and add 1/3 tablespoon of the lemon juice. Add half of the florets and cook for 3 minutes, or until just tender. Remove to a colander and run cold water over them to stop the cooking. Let them drains and set aside.
  2. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over medium-low heat. Add the celery and leeks and cook, stirring, for 5-7 minutes or until the vegetables are tender. Add the flour and stir it in thoroughly, then whisk in the stock until the flour is entirely absorbed. Turn the heat to high and bring to a vigorous boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Chop the uncooked broccoli into small pieces&amp;nbsp; and add to the soup. Simmer for 15 minutes, or until very tender. Process in a blender until almost smooth - you want to retain a slightly grainy texture.
  4. Return the puree to the saucepan, season to taste with salt and pepper and the rest of the lemon juice, then add the cream and the reserved broccoli. Reheat and serve, sprinkled with chives.
