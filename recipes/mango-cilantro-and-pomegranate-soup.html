Mango, Cilantro and Pomegranate Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) mango 1 large or 2 small, ripe
  • 1 unit(s) red chiles seeded and coarsely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) ginger 1/2 inch piece, peeled and finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) cilantro coarsely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) lime(s) juice, 1 to 1-1/2 limes
  • 1.25 cup(s) pomegranate juice or cranberry juice
  • 1 to taste cilantro coarsely chopped to serve
  • 1 to taste pomegranate seeds to serve

Directions

  1. Peel the mango and cut off all the flesh. Add to a blender or food processor along with the chile, ginger, cilantro, lime juice, pomegranate juice, and a little salt and blend to a puree. Taste and add a little more salt if needed.
  2. Pour into small bowls or shot glasses, top with a little more chopped cilantro and a few pomegranate seeds and serve at once.
