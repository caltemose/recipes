Emmental and Beer Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 2 unit(s) onion(s) finely chopped
  • 1 clove(s) garlic chopped
  • 0.33 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 3.25 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 0.67 cup(s) beer German wheat beer
  • 5.5 ounce(s) Emmenthal cheese finely grated
  • 5.5 ounce(s) gruyere cheese finely grated
  • 1 tablespoon(s) thyme fresh, to serve

Directions

  1. Melt the butter over low heat in a large, heavy pan. Add the onion and garlic and saute for 10 minutes until soft but not colored. Gradually stir in the flour, then add the stock a little at a time, stirring constantly until smooth. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes or until the liquid begins to thicken slightly. Stir regularly to be sure the mixture doesn’t start to stick to the pan, then add the beer and return to a gentle boil.
  2. Remove from the heat and very gradually add the two cheeses, adding a handful at a time and stirring constantly. Season to taste with white pepper, pour into warm soup bowls, and top with fresh thyme. Serve with German bread and beer.
