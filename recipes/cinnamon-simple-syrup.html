Cinnamon Simple Syrup

Ingredients

  • 1 cup(s) sugar
  • 1 cup(s) water
  • 4 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s)

Directions

  1. Bring water to a boil. Add sugar and stir until dissolved. Add cinnamon sticks and cover and simmer for 5 minutes. Remove from heat and let steep for 1-6 hours.
