Roasted Thanksgiving Salad

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound(s) sweet potato(es) peeled and cut into 1/4-inch pieces
  • 1 pound(s) parsnip(s) peeled and cupt into 1/4-inch pieces
  • 1 unit(s) red onion(s) small, diced
  • 0.25 cup(s) olive oil plus 2 T
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 cup(s) quinoa rinsed in cool water and drained
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) ground cinnamon
  • 3 tablespoon(s) apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) maple syrup
  • 1 tablespoon(s) Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoon(s) thyme leaves
  • 1 teaspoon(s) sage about 4 leaves, finely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) apple(s) small, Gala or Honeycrisp, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
  • 3 ounce(s) pepitas roasted

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425 F.
  2. Toss the sweet potatoes, parsnips, and red onion with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil and 1 teaspoon of the salt and spread evenly on two baking pans. Roast for 15 minutes. Then toss the vegetables, rotate the pans, and roast for another 10 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender and golden brown. While the vegetables are roasting, prepare the quinoa.
  3. Bring 2 cups water and 1/2 teaspoon of the salt to a boil in a 2-quart saucepan. Whisk the rinsed quinoa into the boiling water, add the cinnamon, cover, reduce the heat to low, and simmer for 15 minutes. Remove from the heat and cool, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
  4. Combine the remaining 1/4 cup olive oil, the vinegar, maple syrup, mustard, and the remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt in a small canning jar. Cover and shake the dressing like a martini to emulsify.
  5. Toss the warm vegetables, quinoa, thyme, sage, and dressing together in a large bowl. Add the apple and pepitas just before serving warm or at room temperature.
