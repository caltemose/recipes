Braised Red Cabbage

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2.5 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) large, julienned
  • 2.5 pound(s) cabbage red, shredded
  • 1 to taste sugar
  • 1 tablespoon(s) salt
  • 1 unit(s) apple(s) medium, sliced
  • 2.5 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 2 cup(s) chicken broth or water
  • 1 stick(s) cinnamon
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 2 unit(s) cloves
  • 5 unit(s) peppercorns crushed
  • 1 tablespoon(s) red currant jelly
  • 1 to taste lemon juice

Directions

  1. Make a sachet for the seasonings: tie together the cinnamon stick, bay leaf, whole cloves, and peppercorns in a cheesecloth bag.
  2. In a dutch oven, heat the oil over medium-low heat. Add the onion and saute until translucent. Stir in the cabbage and cook for 3 minutes. Add the sugar, salt, apple, vinegar, broth or water, and the sachet and bring to a boil over high heat. Cover the pan, reduce the heat to low, and braise for 35 to 60 minutes until the cabbage is tender, stirring occasionally. Stir in the red currant jelly and lemon juice. Remove the sachet before serving.
