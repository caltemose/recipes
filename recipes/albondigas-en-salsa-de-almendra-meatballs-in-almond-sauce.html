Albondigas en Salsa de Almendra (Meatballs in Almond Sauce)

CIA

Ingredients

  • 15 clove(s) garlic
  • 1 cup(s) bread crumbs fresh
  • 1.5 cup(s) dry white wine
  • 0.75 pound(s) ground beef
  • 1 pound(s) ground pork
  • 1 pound(s) ground veal
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 7 tablespoon(s) parsley minced
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) minced
  • 1 unit(s) carrot(s) minced
  • 30 unit(s) almonds whole, blanched
  • 2 cup(s) beef broth
  • 0.75 cup(s) peas
  • 3 unit(s) green onion minced
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf

Directions

  1. Mince 5 of the garlic cloves to a fine paste and set aside. Slice the remaining 10 cloves very thinly and set aside separately.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the bread crumbs with 1/4 cup of the wine and let them soak for 15 minutes. Add the meats, eggs, minced garlic, one-half of the parsley, and salt and pepper. Mix the mixture with a wooden spoon until evenly blended. Shape the mixture into small meatballs.
  3. In a large skillet or flameproof casserole, heat the oil over medium-high heat. In batches if necessary to avoid overcrowding, add the meatballs to the pan and cook until evenly browned on all sides, about 12 to 15 minutes. Transfer the meatballs to a platter.
  4. Add the onion and carrot to the skillet and saute over medium-high heat until the onion is translucent, about 10 minutes. Add the remaining 1-1/4 cups of the wine and stir to scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Add the sliced garlic and continue to cook until the liquid has nearly all reduced.
  5. In a food processor, process the almonds to a fine paste. With the machine running, add the beef broth in a gradual stream until blended. Pour the almond mixture into the pan with the onions and carrots. Bring the mixture to a simmer.
  6. Return the meatballs to the pan. Add the peas, the remaining half of the parsley, the green onions and bay leaf. Bring the sauce back to a bare simmer over low heat and cook for about 45 minutes until the meatballs are cooked through. Taste the sauce and season with salt and pepper. Divide the meatballs and sauce among serving plates. Serve hot.
