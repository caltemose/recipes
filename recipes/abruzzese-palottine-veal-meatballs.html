Abruzzese Palottine (Veal Meatballs)

The Glorious Pasta of Italy

Ingredients

  • 12 ounce(s) veal minced
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 pinch nutmeg freshly ground
  • 1 unit(s) egg(s) large, lightly beaten
  • 1 unit(s) vegetable oil for frying

Directions

  1. Put the veal, salt, nutmeg and egg in a bowl and mix together thoroughly with your hands.
  2. With your fingers, pinch off a very small piece of the mixture and roll it into a tiny ball - not much larger than a chickpea. Place the meatball on a clean tray or platter. Repeat until you have rolled all of the veal mixture into meatballs.
  3. Pour the vegetable oil to a depth of 1/4 inch in a frying pan, place over medium-high heat, and heat to 375 degrees F on a deep-frying thermometer. If you don't have a thermometer, carefully drop a meatball into the hot oil; if it sizzles on contact, the oil is ready.
  4. Place a platter lined with a double layer of paper towels near the stove.
  5. Carefully add the meatballs to the hot oil, working in batches to avoid crowding the pan. Fry them, turning them from time to time with a spatula, slotted spoon, or fork, for about 4 minutes, or until they are lightly browned.
  6. Remove the meatballs to a prepared platter. Repeat until all the meatballs are fried.
  7. Transfer the meatballs to a pot with Abruzzese ragu. Serve over pasta alla chittara.
  8. Meatballs can also be stored in a tight container in the fridge for up to 3 days or in the freezer for up to 1 month.
