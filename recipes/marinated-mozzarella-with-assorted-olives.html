Marinated Mozzarella with Assorted Olives

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 pound(s) mozzarella balls
  • 4 tablespoon(s) oregano minced
  • 18 unit(s) kalamata olive(s)
  • 18 unit(s) olive(s) picholine
  • 18 unit(s) olive(s) dry-cured
  • 18 unit(s) sun-dried tomatoes oil-packed, drained (reserve oil) and halved

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, toss the cheese with the oregano, olives, and tomatoes. Drizzle some of the reserved oil over the salad. Season with salt and pepper.
