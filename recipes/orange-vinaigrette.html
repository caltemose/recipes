Orange Vinaigrette

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup(s) orange juice
  • 0.25 cup(s) white wine vinegar
  • 0.25 cup(s) lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoon(s) Dijon mustard
  • 3 cup(s) olive oil light olive oil or canola oil
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 3 tablespoon(s) fresh herbs minced, chives, parsley, tarragon (optional)

Directions

  1. In a bowl, combine the orange juice, vinegar, lemon juice, and mustard. While whisking, gradually drizzle in the oil until blended. Season the dressing with salt and pepper and stir in the fresh herbs.
