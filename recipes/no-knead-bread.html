No-Knead Bread

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup(s) water
  • 1.25 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 teaspoon(s) yeast
  • 3 cup(s) bread flour or all-purpose flour

Directions

  1. Combine flour, yeast, salt. Add water and stir until blended. Dough will be shaggy, but don’t worry. Cover bowl with plastic wrap and let sit at room temperature for at least 12 hours.
  2. The next day, flour a work surface and roll dough a few times to work out the bubbles. Add flour as needed to the dough if it is sticky.
  3. Coat a smooth towel in flour or cornmeal (be generous with the flour which will save you from laundry headaches). Place dough on towel. Cover with another cotton towel and let rise about 2 hours. When it is ready, dough will be more than double in size and will not readily spring back when poked with a finger.
  4. Preheat oven to 450 degrees with a 6-8 quart heavy pot inside. (Suggesting a dutch oven pot https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/food-network-essentials/what-is-a-dutch-oven)
  5. When oven is hot, remove pot. Gently add dough, cover with lid. Bake in oven for 30 minutes.
  6. Remove cover and bake another 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool before slicing.
