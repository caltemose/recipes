Orange Oil

CIA

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 4 unit(s) orange peel large

Directions

  1. In a small, heavy pot, heat the oils over low heat until they reach 140 F. Add the orange zest. Pour the oil into a glass jar. Cover and refrigerate for 24 hours. Remove the pieces of zest.
