Pumpkin Mascarpone Cheesecake

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.25 cup(s) graham cracker crumbs
  • 2 tablespoon(s) sugar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter melted
  • 1 teaspoon(s) egg white(s)
  • 1.5 pound(s) cream cheese room temperature
  • 1 cup(s) brown sugar plus 1 T, packed
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground ginger
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) ground cinnamon
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground mace
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 9 ounce(s) mascarpone
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s)

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 325 F. Butter the sides and bottom of a 10-inch cake pan and line the bottom with a circle of parchment paper.
  2. In a shallow pan, blend the graham cracker crumbs and sugar evenly with a fork. Add the melted butter and egg white and mix well. Press the graham mixture into the cake pan to make an even bottom crust. Bake the crust until it is lightly toasted and set, about 8 minutes. Cool slightly.
  3. In a bowl, combine the cream cheese and brown sugar. With a mixer on low speed, or with a wooden spoon by hand, blend the ingredients until very smooth. Take care not to overbeat the mixture or you will incorporate too much air into the cheesecake.
  4. Add the pumpkin puree, ginger, cinnamon, mace, and salt and mix gently for 3 to 4 minutes, scraping down the sides and bottom of the bowl often, until the mixture is evenly blended. Add the mascarpone and mix gently for 2 to 3 minutes, scraping the bowl. Add the eggs and mix for 2 to 3 minutes, scraping the bowl as necessary.
  5. Pour the pumpkin filling over the crust. Place the cake pan in a deep baking dish and set this assembly on the oven rack. Add enough boiling water to the baking pan to come two-thirds to three-fourths of the way up the sides of the cake pan.
  6. Bake the cake for 1-1/4 hours, or until the center of the cake is lightly set. Carefully remove the cheesecake from the baking pan and cool it to room temperature. Refrigerate the cake for at least 8 hours before unmolding.
  7. Carefully lower the can pan into a pan of hot water for a few seconds. Invert the cake pan onto a plate, then invert the cake onto a serving plate.
  8. Dip a sharp knife with a long, thin blade into very hot water. Wipe the blade dry and make a cut. For the neatest slices, repeat this process for each separate cut that is made.
