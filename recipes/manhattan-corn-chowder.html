Manhattan Corn Chowder

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 4 ounce(s) bacon finely diced
  • 0.5 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) finely diced
  • 0.5 unit(s) orange bell pepper(s) finely diced
  • 1.5 unit(s) yellow onion(s) medium, finely diced
  • 3 clove(s) garlic thinly sliced
  • 2 stalk(s) celery finely diced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) thyme minced
  • 2 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 2 teaspoon(s) oregano dried
  • 2 cup(s) corn
  • 2 cup(s) potato small dice
  • 2.5 quart(s) chicken broth
  • 1.5 cup(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and diced
  • 0.25 cup(s) olive oil for garnish
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley coarsely chopped, for garnish
  • 0.33 cup(s) parmeggiano grated, for garnish

Directions

  1. In a 4-quart saucepan, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the bacon and cook until the fat is rendered and it begins to turn brown and crisp.
  2. Add the peppers, onions, garlic, and celery to the pan and saute over low heat until the vegetables are softened and the onions are translucent.
  3. Add the thyme, bay, oregano, corn, potatoes, and broth to the pan and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to low and simmer until the potatoes and the corn are tender, about 20 minutes. Add the tomatoes and season the soup with salt and pepper.
  4. Ladle portions into heated bowls or soup plates garnished with olive oil, parsley, and parmesan, if desired.
