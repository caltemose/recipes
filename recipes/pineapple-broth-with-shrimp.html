Pineapple Broth with Shrimp

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) diced
  • 1.75 ounce(s) peanuts raw, skinned
  • 2 tablespoon(s) coconut flakes
  • 1.25 inch ginger chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) coriander seeds
  • 2 teaspoon(s) sesame seeds
  • 1 teaspoon(s) cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon(s) poppy seeds
  • 3 tablespoon(s) tamarind pulp
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) turmeric
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) chili powder
  • 2 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon(s) sugar palm sugar, or brown sugar
  • 1.75 cup(s) fish stock
  • 1 cup(s) pineapple juice
  • 1.75 cup(s) coconut milk
  • 1 cup(s) shrimp chopped
  • 4.5 ounce(s) pineapple chopped
  • 1 unit(s) lime(s) juiced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) mint coarsely torn
  • 2 teaspoon(s) peanuts raw, toasted and chopped

Directions

  1. Heat a cast-iron pan. Add the onion, peanuts, coconut, ginger, and all the seeds. Stir over low heat until the coconut darkens and spices turn aromatic, about 5 minutes. Transfer to a bowl to cool, then place in a food processor and process to a coarse paste. Add the tamarind pulp, turmeric, chili powder, and salt and pepper, and process to a smooth paste.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large saucepan and cook the paste for about 10 minutes over low heat. Stir in the sugar and continue cooking until the paste begins to stick to the bottom of the pan. Pour in the stock and pineapple juice and simmer for 20 minutes, partially covered.
  3. Remove the soup from the heat and process in a blender until smooth. You might need to do this in batches. Stir in the coconut milk and return the pan to the heat. Once it begins to bubble, add the shrimp and simmer for 3 minutes. Add the lime juice and stir in the pineapple pieces and mint leaves. Ladle into bowls and scatter the peanuts over the top.
