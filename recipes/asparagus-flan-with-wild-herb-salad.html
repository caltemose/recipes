Asparagus Flan with Wild Herb Salad

Fran's Olive Oil Cookboo

Ingredients

  • 7 ounce(s) asparagus
  • 6.5 ounce(s) ricotta fresh, cow's milk
  • 2 ounce(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 2 ounce(s) pecorino grated
  • 3 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 1 clove(s) garlic
  • 0.5 ounce(s) fresh herbs basil, oregano, marjoram
  • 4 ounce(s) wild herb greens
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 1 unit(s) semolina for the dish

Directions

  1. Wash the asparagus and break off the fibrous ends. Cut into halves and cook in boiling salted water for about 5 minutes. Drain off the water and allow the asparagus to cool. Preheat the oven to 170 C (340 F) with fan. Oil 8 metal molds each with a capacity of 100 ml (1/2 C) and dust with a little semolina.
  2. With a stick mixer, blend the asparagus, ricotta, eggs, garlic and herbs and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Fill the molds three-quarters full with the asparagus mixture and place them in a high-sided oven-proof tray. Fill with water to half way up the molds and bake in a water bath for 30 minutes on the second rack from the bottom of the oven.
  4. Switch off the oven and allow the molds to rest in the oven for about 10 minutes.
  5. In the meantime, wash the greens for the salad and drizzle with lemon juice, olive oil, salt and pepper. Arrange the salad on plates and add 2 asparagus flans on each plate. Drizzle with a little olive oil. If desired, sprinkle some dried petals over the plates.
