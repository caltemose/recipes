Chipotle-Sherry Vinaigrette #2

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup(s) dry sherry vinegar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon(s) shallot(s) minced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) thyme chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped
  • 2 unit(s) canned chipotle chiles drained and minced
  • 1 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 1 teaspoon(s) maple syrup
  • 1 cup(s) olive oil plus 2 T

Directions

  1. In a medium bowl, combine all of the ingredients except the olive oil. Gradually whisk in the olive oil until the dressing is lightly thickened. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
