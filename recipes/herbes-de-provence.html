Herbes de Provence

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) oregano dried
  • 1 unit(s) dill dried
  • 1 unit(s) chervil dried
  • 2 unit(s) fennel seeds
  • 2 unit(s) marjoram dried
  • 3 unit(s) thyme dried
  • 3 unit(s) rosemary dried
  • 3 unit(s) tarragon dried
  • 3 unit(s) basil dried

Directions

  1. Seal in a jar and shake. Will keep for about 2 months.
