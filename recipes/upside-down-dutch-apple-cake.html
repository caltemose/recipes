Upside-Down Dutch Apple Cake

CIA

Ingredients

  • 6 unit(s) apple(s) baking apples, peeled
  • 1 to taste cinnamon sugar
  • 1 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 0.75 cup(s) sugar
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 0.5 cup(s) butter melted
  • 1 teaspoon(s) vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 F. Cut the apples into wedges and arrange them in the bottom of a well-buttered 10-inch cast-iron skillet. Sprinkle the apples with cinnamon sugar.
  2. In a bowl, with a mixer or by hand, combine the flour and sugar. Add the eggs, butter, and vanilla to the flour mixture and mix until a smooth batter forms. Pour the batter evenly over the apples.
  3. Bake the cake for 45 minutes or until the batter is cooked through and browned on top. Remove the pan from the oven and cool the cake for 30 minutes. Invert the cake onto a serving plate. Cut into wedges and serve warm.
