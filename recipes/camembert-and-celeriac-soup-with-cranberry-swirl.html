Camembert and Celeriac Soup with Cranberry Swirl

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter 2-3 T
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) chopped
  • 0.5 unit(s) celeriac small, about 8 oz, coarsely diced
  • 2 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 1 unit(s) bouquet garni
  • 2 cup(s) milk
  • 4 ounce(s) camembert diced
  • 4 tablespoon(s) heavy cream
  • 4 teaspoon(s) cranberry sauce
  • 4 teaspoon(s) orange juice or cranberry
  • 4 teaspoon(s) vegetable oil sunflower

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan. Add the onion and cook gently, stirring for 2 minutes, or until softened but not browned. Add the celeriac, stock, bouquet garni, a little salt, and lots of pepper. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, cover, and simmer gently for 15 minutes, or until the celeriac is tender. Remove and discard the bouquet garni.
  2. Puree the mixture in a blender or food processor, adding the milk and cheese. Return the soup to the rinsed-out pan, stir in the cream, and reheat, but do not boil. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  3. Meanwhile, whisk the cranberry sauce with the juice and oil until thoroughly blended. Ladle the soup into warm, shallow soup bowls. Whisk the cranberry mixture again and, using a teaspoon, swill a little of the mixture in the center of each bowl and quickly draw a toothpick from the center to the edge all around to form a pinwheel design. Serve immediately.
