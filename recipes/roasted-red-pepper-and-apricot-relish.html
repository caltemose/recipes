Roasted Red Pepper and Apricot Relish

CIA

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 1 cup(s) red onion(s) finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) garlic minced
  • 1 cup(s) red bell pepper(s) roasted, peeled, and finely diced
  • 1 cup(s) dried apricots diced
  • 0.5 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 3 teaspoon(s) white wine vinegar 3-4 t
  • 1 teaspoon(s) honey mustard
  • 3 dash Tabasco sauce
  • 1 teaspoon(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. In a heavy, medium saucepan over medium heat, heat the oil and saute the onion and garlic for 1 minute. Add the red peppers, apricots, and broth and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
  2. Add the vinegar, mustard, and Tabasco. Cook until most of the liquid has evaporated. Add black pepper to taste.
  3. Serve either at room temperature or chilled. Add the parsley just before serving.
