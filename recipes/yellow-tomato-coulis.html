Yellow Tomato Coulis

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) olive oil
  • 0.25 cup(s) onion(s) minced
  • 1 teaspoon(s) garlic minced
  • 1.5 pound(s) tomato(es) yellow, quartered
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) sugar
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 dash Tabasco sauce
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf

Directions

  1. In a large skillet, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and sauce until the onion is translucent. Add the tomatoes, sugar, salt, tabasco, and bay leaf, and simmer until the mixture appears dry, about 30 minutes.
  2. Remove the bay leaf and process the mixture in a blender until smooth. Strain the coulis through a wire mesh sieve and keep warm.&nbsp; Cookbook suggests to serve it around seared salmon and with corn, potato, and arugula salad.
