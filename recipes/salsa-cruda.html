Salsa Cruda

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2.5 cup(s) tomato(es) seeded and diced
  • 0.5 cup(s) onion(s) minced
  • 0.5 cup(s) bell pepper(s) diced
  • 2 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 0.5 cup(s) cilantro chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) oregano mexican, chopped
  • 2 unit(s) lime(s) juiced
  • 1 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) seeded and minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) black pepper or to taste
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt or to taste

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a food processor and pulse until the desired consistency is achieved. For a chunkier consistency, mix the ingredients by hand. Taste and adjust the seasonings. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
