Spicy Vegetable Saute with Cucumber Raita and Beet Chutney

CIA

Ingredients

  • 3 cup(s) broccoli florets
  • 1.5 cup(s) cauliflower florets
  • 0.75 cup(s) rutabaga diced
  • 0.75 cup(s) carrot(s) thickly sliced
  • 2 teaspoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon(s) mustard seeds
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) cumin seeds
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) black onion seeds
  • 0.33 cup(s) onion(s) diced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) ginger minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) jalapeño pepper(s) minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) garlic minced
  • 4 unit(s) red chiles dried
  • 1 to taste cucumber raita
  • 1 to taste beet chutney

Directions

  1. Steam the broccoli, cauliflower, rutabaga and carrots separately until each is barely tender. Refrigerate the steamed vegetables until needed.
  2. When read to serve, heat the oil in a large sauce pan over medium-high heat and add the mustard seeds, onion seeds, and cumin seeds. When the seeds begin to pop, add the reserved broccoli, cauliflower, rutabaga, carrots, and diced onion and saute and heat until the vegetables are heated through, about 3 minutes. Add the ginger, jalapeño, garlic, and dried chiles and saute for 5 minutes. Discard the chiles.
  3. Serve the vegetables garnished with cucumber raita and beet chutney.
