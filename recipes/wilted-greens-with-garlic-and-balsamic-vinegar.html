Wilted Greens with Garlic and Balsamic Vinegar

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 10 cup(s) assorted greens chard, mustard, escarole, chicory, radicchio, turnip, beets
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 tablespoon(s) balsamic vinegar
  • 2 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 1 pinch red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Rinse the greens carefully in several changes of water and then dry well. Cut into strips 1 inch wide.
  2. In a large saucepan over medium-high heat, warm the olive oil. Add the greens and, using tongs, toss them until they are wilted but still retain their bright color, 3-4 minutes.
  3. Add the vinegar, garlic, red pepper flakes, and salt and pepper to taste, and toss well. Transfer to a serving dish and serve immediately.
