Weeknight Spaghetti

Ingredients

  • 16 ounce(s) olive oil
  • 6 clove(s) garlic large, crushed
  • 3 sprig(s) thyme
  • 2 sprig(s) rosemary
  • 10 leave(s) basil plus more for serving
  • 1 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes
  • 1 can(s) tomato(es) 28 oz can, whole tomatoes, drained
  • 1 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 unit(s) anchovies 1-2 fish
  • 0.5 pound(s) dry pasta
  • 1 tablespoon(s) salt
  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 1 to taste parmeggiano

Directions

  1. Combine the olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, basil, and red pepper flakes in a narrow saucepan over medium heat. As the heat builds, there will be considerable bubbling as the water cooks out of the garlic and herbs. When this dies down and the basil and garlic turn brown, kill the heat and let the mixture steep until cool. Strain the oil into a jar and toss the solids. Use immediately or cover tightly and freeze to keep the oil stable and safe. Will keep safely in the freezer for 2 months. If left at room temperature, the steeped oil can make spores that can cause botulism.
  2. Heat 3 tablespoons of the herb oil in a wide saute pan over medium heat. Add the tomatoes and salt to the pan and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, breaking up the tomatoes as they soften by using a wooden spatula or a potato masher.
  3. Reduce the heat, stir in the anchovies, and cook for another 5 minutes or until the anchovies completely vanish from view.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the pasta using the Cold Water Pasta Method.
  5. Fish the spaghetti out of the water with tongs or a hand strainer, allowing most but not all of the water to drain away. Stir the pasta into the sauce and cook on low for 5 minutes. Finish with the butter, parmesan, and fresh basil. If the sauce seems too dry, add 1/4 cup of the pasta water and serve.
