Winter Squash Soup with Basil and Lime Cream

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 5 clove(s) garlic
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) large, chopped
  • 1 inch ginger peeled and coarsely chopped
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 3.25 pound(s) winter squash pumpkin, butternut, acorn, seeds and rinds removed and coarsely cubed
  • 2 sprig(s) parsley
  • 1 sprig(s) thyme
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 3 unit(s) star anise
  • 12 ounce(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and coarsely chopped
  • 2 quart(s) vegetable stock hot
  • 1.25 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 1 unit(s) lime(s) juice of 1 to 2
  • 1 handful(s) basil chopped, to serve

Directions

  1. Chop two of the garlic cloves, but leave the rest whole. Put the chopped garlic and the whole garlic into a large pan with the onion, ginger, oil, and squash. Tie the herbs together in a bundle and toss those in. Cover with a lid and cook over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring once or twice.
  2. Add the star anise, tomatoes, and stock, then season with salt and freshly ground black pepper and bring to a boil. Simmer for 20 minutes or until the squash is tender. Pick out and discard the bundle of herbs and the star anise. Process the soup in a blender until smooth and then pass it through a sieve. Taste and adjust the seasoning, then reheat gently.
  3. Meanwhile, pour the cream into a bowl, add the lime juice, and whisk. Once the cream holds its shape loosely, stir in the basil. As you ladle the soup into bowls, top with a floating crown of basil and lime cream.
