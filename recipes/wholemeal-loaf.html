Wholemeal Loaf

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1.67 cup(s) bread flour plus more for kneading
  • 1.67 cup(s) whole wheat flour plus more to dust
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) yeast
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.75 cup(s) water hot
  • 0.75 cup(s) milk whole
  • 1 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil plus more for the bowl
  • 1 tablespoon(s) honey
  • 1 unit(s) egg(s) large, beaten, to glas

Directions

  1. Mix the bread flour, whole wheat flour, yeast, and salt in a large bowl. Make a well in the center. Stir the hot water, milk, oil, and honey together and pour into the well. Mix to form a slightly sticky dough. Cover with plastic wrap and let stand for 10 minutes.
  2. Mix the bread flour, whole wheat flour, yeast, and salt in a large bowl. Make a well in the center. Stir the hot water, milk, oil, and honey together and pour into the well. Mix to form a slightly sticky dough. Cover with plastic wrap and let stand for 10 minutes.
  3. Oil and flour a 8x5 loaf pan, and tap out the excess flour. Briefly knead the dough on the work surface. Press the dough into a rough rectangle and place in the pan. Cover loosely with oiled plastic wrap. Let stand in a warm place for 30 minutes, or until doubled in size.
  4. Preheat the oven to 425 F (220 C). Brush the egg over the loaf to glaze it, and sprinkle with a little whole wheat flour. Slash a few diagonal slits in the top of the loaf. Bake for 20 minutes. Reduce the oven temperature to 400 F (200 C) and bake for 20 minutes, or until the loaf sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom. Let cool on a wire rack for 10 minutes. Invert and unfold onto the rack and cool completely.
