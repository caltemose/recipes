Chicken Breasts with Mustard, Mortadella, and Parmesan

CIA

Ingredients

  • 3 unit(s) chicken breasts boneless, skinless, halved
  • 6 leave(s) sage chopped
  • 1 unit(s) all-purpose flour for dredging
  • 0.25 cup(s) olive oil
  • 2 tablespoon(s) Dijon mustard
  • 6 slice(s) mortadella
  • 1 unit(s) parmeggiano slices
  • 1.5 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoon(s) sage chopped
  • 1.5 cup(s) butter cut into small pieces

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Sprinkle the chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and sage. Dredge them in the flour.
  2. In a large saucepan or skillet over high heat, heat the olive oil. Add the chicken (do not crowd the pan) and cook until lightly browned on each side, turning once.
  3. Transfer the chicken to a baking dish and bake for 12&nbsp; to 15 minutes or until opaque throughout.
  4. Meanwhile, to make the sauce: In a large, heavy saucepan, simmer the broth until it is reduced by three quarters, about 10 minutes. Add the sage, salt, and pepper. Gradually whisk in the butter until the sauce is creamy. Set aside and keep warm.
  5. Preheat the broiler. Meanwhile, brush the chicken on top with the mustard. Place a slice of mortadella and cheese on each piece. Place the chicken under the broiler and cook until the cheese is lightly browned, about 2 minutes. Serve at once with the mixed vegetables and sauce.
