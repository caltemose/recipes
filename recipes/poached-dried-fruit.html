Poached Dried Fruit

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup(s) sugar
  • 1.5 cup(s) water
  • 1 unit(s) vanilla bean 1-inch, split in half
  • 1 unit(s) lemon(s) juiced
  • 1.75 cup(s) dried fruit apricots, currants, cherries, raisins

Directions

  1. In a medium saucepan, combine the sugar, water and vanilla bean and lemon juice. Bring to a boil and pour over the dried fruit. Let the fruit sit for 20 to 30 minutes or until the fruit is plump and tender.
  2. Serve at once or cover and store in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Store the fruit in the syrup until you are ready to serve it. If refrigerated, let sit at room temperature for 30 minutes before serving. To serve, remove the fruit from the syrup with a slotted spoon.
