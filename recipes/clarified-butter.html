Clarified Butter

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 4 stick(s) butter

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a narrow, heavy saucepan over low heat until all bubbles dissipate (that is, until the water has cooked out) and the liquid clears, 30 to 40 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer, being careful to leave any solids at the bottom of the pan.
  2. Keep tightly covered to keep the fat from absorbing flavors from the fridge or freezer.&amp;nbsp; Will keep in a jar in the fridge for up to six months or up to a year in the freezer.
