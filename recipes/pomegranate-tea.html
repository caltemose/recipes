Pomegranate Tea

The Indian Vegan Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3 cup(s) water
  • 4 unit(s) cardamom crushed open
  • 1 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s)
  • 2 unit(s) black tea or 2 t tea leaves
  • 1 cup(s) pomegranate juice
  • 1 to taste sugar

Directions

  1. Boil water with cardamom pods and cinnamon stick. Add tea bags, pomegranate juice, and sugar.
  2. Remove tea bags and strain. Serve hot or cold over ice.
