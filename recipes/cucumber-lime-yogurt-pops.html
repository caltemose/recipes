Cucumber Lime Yogurt Pops

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 1 pound(s) cucumber(s) seeded and chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) greek yogurt
  • 0.5 cup(s) demerara sugar
  • 1 unit(s) lime(s) large, zested and juiced
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) chili powder
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt

Directions

  1. Combine the cucumbers, yogurt, sugar, lime zest, lime juice, chile powder, and salt in a food processor. Puree for 30 seconds, stop, scrape down the slides of the bowl, and puree until smooth.
  2. Pour the mixture into popsicle molds, add the popsicle sticks, and freeze for 4 hours or until solid. Store in a zip-top bag in the freezer for up to 2 weeks.
