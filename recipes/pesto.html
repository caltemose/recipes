Pesto

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup(s) basil lightly packed, carefully washed and well dried
  • 0.25 cup(s) parsley carefully washed and well dried
  • 4 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 0.5 cup(s) olive oil
  • 1.25 cup(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 0.25 cup(s) pine nuts optional

Directions

  1. In a blender or food processor fitted with the metal blade, combine the basil, parsley, garlic, (pine nuts), olive oil, and about half of the parmesan. Process for 1 minute, stopping to scrape down the sides of the container as needed, and continuing to process until smooth.
  2. Add the remaining cheese and pulse a few times to combine. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
