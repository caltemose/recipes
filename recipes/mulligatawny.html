Mulligatawny

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2.5 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) chopped
  • 2 inch ginger finely chopped
  • 2 clove(s) garlic finely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) apple(s) diced with peel
  • 1 unit(s) carrot(s) sliced
  • 1 stalk(s) celery sliced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) curry powder heaping T
  • 1 tablespoon(s) all-purpose flour
  • 4 unit(s) tomato(es) coarsely chopped
  • 2 teaspoon(s) tomato puree
  • 2 cup(s) chicken broth hot
  • 1 unit(s) red chiles split
  • 2 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 0.75 cup(s) coconut milk or half and half
  • 7 ounce(s) chicken cooked, shredded
  • 2 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped
  • 0.5 unit(s) lime(s) juiced

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a large pan and soften the onion, ginger, and garlic for 10 minutes without coloring. Add the apple, carrot, and celery to the pan and continue cooking, covered, for 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the curry powder and cook for 1 minute, stirring all the time. Sprinkle in the flour and continue cooking for another 20 seconds.
  3. Add the tomatoes and tomato puree followed by the chicken stock and red chile. Add the bay leaves and season with salt and pepper. Bring to a boil, stirring, then reduce the heat and simmer, half-covered, for 20 minutes.
  4. Remove the bay leaf and chile and blend until smooth, using a stick blender. Sieve the soup to remove any fibers and skin. Reheat, stir in the coconut milk or half and half, and add the diced chicken. Sharpen with a squeeze of lime and add the chopped cilantro.
