Smoky Tequila Sour

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) lime(s)
  • 1 ounce(s) tequila agave silver or blanco
  • 1 ounce(s) montenegro
  • 0.5 unit(s) orange(s) small, 1-1/2 ounce juice
  • 1 tablespoon(s) agave nectar light nectar
  • 1 dash liquid smoke or use smoky mezcal instead of tequila

Directions

  1. Fill an old-fashioned with ice and set aside.
  2. Cut a wedge from one of the limes and reserve, then juice both limes into a cocktail shaker. Add tequila, Montenegro, orange juice, agave nectar and half a dozen or so ice cubes. Cover and shake for 30 seconds.
  3. Strain the mixture through the cocktail strainer into the glass. Add liquid smoke, garnish with lime wedge.
