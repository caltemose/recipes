Lasagne di Crespelle with Truffled Mushrooms

The Glorious Pasta of Italy

Ingredients

  • 1 ounce(s) dried porcini
  • 1 cup(s) water boiling
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon(s) butter plus more for greasing the dish
  • 1 clove(s) garlic sliced paper thin
  • 1.5 pound(s) mushrooms fresh, mixed variety, coarsely chopped
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 0.5 cup(s) dry white wine
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley minced
  • 24 unit(s) crespelle at room temperature
  • 1 pound(s) mozzarella fresh, cut into small cubes
  • 1.5 cup(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 1 batch tomato sauce heated to a simmer

Directions

  1. Put the porcini in a small heatproof bowl and pour the boiling water over them. Let stand for 20-30 minutes, or until softened.
  2. Drain the porcini in a fine-mesh sieve lined with damp paper towels or cheesecloth, reserving the liquid. Chop the mushrooms coarsely and set the mushrooms and liquid aside separately.
  3. Put the olive oil, 1 T butter, and garlic in a large frying pan on medium heat. When the butter is melted, reduce the heat to medium-low and sautéed the garlic for about 5 minutes, or until softened but not browned.
  4. Add the porcini and the fresh mushrooms and sprinkler with 1 t salt and some pepper. Stir the mushrooms with a wooden spoon or spatular to coat them with the oil and butter. Sauce for about 20 minutes, or until the mushrooms are tender and the liquid they release has evaporated.
  5. Raise the heat to high and pour in the wine and porcini liquid. Stir, and then let the liquid bubble for a minute or so. Reduce the heat to medium-low and continue to cook for about 5 minutes, or until most of the liquid has evaporated. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt or pepper if needed. Remove from the heat and stir in the parsley.
  6. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F. Lightly coat a 12-inch round or 9x13 baking pan with butter.
  7. Arrange 4 or 5 crespelle in the prepared dish, overlapping them where necessary to form a thin layer that covers as much of the bottom of the dish as possible. Spoon one-fourth of the mushrooms over the crespelle and spread them out gently in an even layer. Sprinkler one-fourth of the mozzarella and a little of the parmiggiano over the mushrooms, and spoon a thin layer of the sauce over all.
  8. Make 3 more layers this way. Top with a fifth layer of crespelle and spoon a final layer of sauce over them. Sprinkler with the remaining parmiggiano.
  9. Bake the lasagne for 25-30 minutes, or until it is hot and bubbly and then cheese on top is melted and lightly browned. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving. Serve immediately.
  10. The lasagne may be assembled and stored, unbaked, in the fridge for up to 2 days or in the freezer for up to 1 month. If frozen, remove from the freezer and let thaw overnight in the fridge. Bring the dish to room temperate before baking.
