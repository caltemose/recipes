Cannoli Moderna

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoon(s) sugar plus more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon(s) cocoa powder unsweetened, plus more for dusting
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 0.25 cup(s) marsala wine
  • 1 pound(s) ricotta
  • 8 ounce(s) almonds toasted
  • 8 ounce(s) semi-sweet chocolate chopped
  • 6 tablespoon(s) amaretto
  • 1 to taste powdered sugar optional

Directions

  1. Sift the flour, sugar, and cocoa powder. In a food processor or working by hand, add the oil and wine to the sifted dry ingredients and mix until the dough comes together in a ball. Wrap the dough with plastic wrap and let it stand at room temperature for 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 450 F. With a rolling pin or pasta machine, roll out the dough into thin sheets about 1/8-inch thick. With a 2-1/2 to 3-inch cutter, cut the dough into eighteen circles. Transfer the circles to a well-greased baking sheet.
  3. Top the dough circles with a second baking sheet. Place the baking sheets in the oven and bake the circles until golden brown and blistered, about 15 to 18 minutes. Transfer the circles to a rack to cool.
  4. In a food processor or by hand, mix the ricotta, almonds, chocolate, and amaretto until evenly blended. Taste the filling and sweeten to taste with sugar.
  5. Place the filling mixture in a pastry bag without a tip. For each portion, place 1 cannoli circle on a dessert plate. Pipe some of the filling mixture on top of the circle and top with another cannoli circle. Pipe on another layer of filling and top with a third cannoli circle. Dust the top layer with powdered sugar and/or cocoa powder. Serve with chocolate sauce if desired.
