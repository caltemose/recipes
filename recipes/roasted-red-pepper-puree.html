Roasted Red Pepper Puree

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) seeded, roasted, and peeled
  • 2 teaspoon(s) olive oil
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) balsamic vinegar
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. In a blender or food processor, puree the pepper, oil, and vinegar. Add the salt and cayenne.
