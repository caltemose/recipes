Kimchi Crab Cakes

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup(s) mayonnaise
  • 1 unit(s) lime(s) zested and then cut into 6 wedges
  • 1 unit(s) egg(s) large
  • 3 ounce(s) kimchi drained and finely chopped
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 3 ounce(s) bread crumbs panko
  • 8 ounce(s) crabmeat lump
  • 8 ounce(s) crabmeat jumbo lump
  • 0.25 cup(s) vegetable oil canola

Directions

  1. Set a cooling rack inside a sheet pan and set aside.
  2. Whisk the mayonnaise, lime zest, egg, kimchi, pepper, and salt together in a large bowl, then fold in 1-1/2 ounces of the panko and the lump and jumbo lump crabmeat. Divide the crab mixture into twelve 2-ounce portions and shape into patties. Coat the crab cakes in the panko, then place on the prepared cooling rack. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat the canola oil in a 12-inch saute pan over medium heat. When the oil shimmers, cook the crab cakes, 4 at a time, for 3 minutes per side, or until golden brown. Remove to a clean cooling rack set over paper towels to drain. Repeat with the remaining crab cakes.
  4. Serve immediately with the lime wedges.
