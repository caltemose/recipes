Tossed Beet Salad

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup(s) red wine vinegar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) honey
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.25 cup(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) red onion(s) small, thinly sliced
  • 1 unit(s) fennel bulb(s) cored, halved, and sliced wafer thin
  • 1 unit(s) pear(s) Asian, halved, cored and sliced wafer thin
  • 1 unit(s) jicama peeled and cut into matchsticks
  • 2 unit(s) beet(s) large, cooked, peeled and cut into matchsticks
  • 6 ounce(s) goat cheese crumbled

Directions

  1. To make the vinaigrette, whisk the vinegar, lemon juice, honey, pepper and salt together in a large bowl. Keep whisking and slowly drizzle in the olive oil so that the mixture emulsifies.
  2. To prepare the salad, add the red onion and fennel to the bowl and toss to combine.
  3. Add the pear and jicama, toss to coat, and set aside for 10 minutes.
  4. Finally, add the beets and goat cheese and gently toss before serving.
