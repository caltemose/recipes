No-Can Tomato Soup

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) red onion(s) small, chopped
  • 4 clove(s) garlic smashed
  • 6 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 can(s) tomato(es) 28-oz cans, diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup(s) orange juice
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground cinnamon
  • 2 teaspoon(s) dry sherry vinegar

Directions

  1. Sweat the red onions and garlic in 3 tablespoons of olive oil in a pressure cooker over medium-low heat until translucent.
  2. Add the tomatoes, orange juice, salt, and cinnamon and affix the lid. Increase the heat to high. Once the cooker comes to pressure, reduce the heat to low and maintain an even, low hiss for 6 minutes.
  3. Kill the heat and release the pressure on the cooker.
  4. Remove the lid and add the sherry vinegar. Puree with an immersion blender while adding the 3 tablespoons of the remaining olive oil in a steady stream.
  5. Freeze the extra in a zip-top bag. Laying the bag flat to freeze saves space and leads to faster thawing.
