Mango and Shrimp Broth

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon(s) soy sauce
  • 2 teaspoon(s) fish sauce
  • 1 tablespoon(s) sesame oil toasted
  • 1 tablespoon(s) mirin or sake
  • 1 teaspoon(s) sugar
  • 1 unit(s) lime(s) juiced
  • 1 pound(s) shrimp cut into 1 inch pieces
  • 2 stalk(s) lemongrass finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 4 unit(s) red chiles bird's eye, finely sliced
  • 4 unit(s) scallions finely sliced
  • 2 inch ginger finely shredded
  • 4 clove(s) garlic coarsely chopped
  • 4 unit(s) mango small, green, peeled and finely chopped
  • 2 teaspoon(s) brown sugar or palm sugar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) rice wine vinegar
  • 1 quart(s) fish stock
  • 8 unit(s) kaffir lime leaves torn in half
  • 1 tablespoon(s) fish sauce
  • 4 ounce(s) egg noodles medium
  • 4 ounce(s) green beans thin, halved
  • 1 to taste lime juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) mint shredded

Directions

  1. Combine the soy and fish sauce, sesame oil, Kirin, sugar, and lime juice, and spoon over the shrimp. Refrigerate for 20 minutes. Pound the lemongrass to a paste with a dash of water, using a mortar and pestle. Set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok or large pan and cook the chiles, scallions, ginger, and garlic for 30 seconds over high heat. Add the mangoes and cook for 1 minute. Stir in the sugar until it begins to caramelize. Add the vinegar, lemongrass, stock, lime leaves, and fish sauce. Bring to a boil.
  3. Stir in the noodles, beans, and shrimp (not the marinating liquid). Simmer for 3-5 minutes, until the noodles and shrimp are cooked. Season with salt, sharpen with lime juice, and add the herbs.
