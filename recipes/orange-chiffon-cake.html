Orange Chiffon Cake

CIA

Ingredients

  • 3.5 cup(s) cake flour
  • 5.25 teaspoon(s) baking powder
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 2.67 cup(s) sugar
  • 1 cup(s) vegetable oil scant 1 cup
  • 10 unit(s) egg yolks
  • 1 cup(s) water
  • 2 unit(s) orange(s) juice and zest
  • 7 unit(s) egg white(s)
  • 0.67 cup(s) sugar scant 2/3 cup
  • 1 pinch cream of tartar
  • 1 to taste powdered sugar

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Grease and flour two 7-inch ring pans.
  2. Sift together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar. Combine the oil, egg yolks, water, orange juice, and zest. Add the liquid ingredients to the dry ingredients and blend until smooth.
  3. In a clean, oil-free bowl, whip the egg whites with a mixer or by hand with the sugar and cream of tartar until medium-soft peaks form. With a rubber spatula, gradually fold the egg whites into the cake base, taking care not to overmix.
  4. Pour the batter into the prepared pans and bake for 45 minutes or until a cake tester inserted into the center of the cakes comes out clean. Remove the pans from the oven, carefully invert the pans, and cool completely.
  5. When cool, release the cakes from the sides of the pan with a spatula and invert onto serving plates. Dust the cakes with powdered sugar and cut into serving pieces.
