Hollandaise Sauce - Microwave

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) egg yolks large
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon juice or to taste
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 0.25 cup(s) butter melted

Directions

  1. Whisk the egg yolks in a microwave-safe bowl. Add the lemon juice, salt, and cayenne pepper and beat until smooth. Slowly stream the melted butter into the egg mixture while whisking to incorporate.
  2. Heat the sauce in the microwave on medium for 15-20 seconds. Whisk until well combined.
  3. If the sauce cools and separates, whisk again to combine. If needed, add a tablespoon of water to help it combine.
