Cod and Potato Fritters with Aioli

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) cut into small dice
  • 2 pound(s) salt cod
  • 6 pound(s) potato russet
  • 4 cup(s) bread crumbs 4 to 4-1/2 C, dried
  • 1 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 4 unit(s) egg(s) beaten until frothy
  • 5 unit(s) lemon(s) zest and juice
  • 1 bunch dill minced leaves
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 0.75 cup(s) self-rising flour
  • 3 unit(s) egg(s) beaten with 2 T water, for egg wash
  • 1 unit(s) aioli

Directions

  1. In a small skillet, heat 1/4 cup o f the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and sauce until translucent. Cool to room temperature.
  2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Rinse the salt cod in cold water to remove the excess salt. Drop the salt cod into the boiling water and boil for 15 to 20 minutes, until it is soft. While the cod is cooking, simmer the potatoes in salted water until tender.
  3. Drain the salt cod and cool it until just cool enough to handle, but still quite warm. With your fingertips, shred the cod into fine pieces, removing any bones or cartilage, and place them in a bowl. Drain the potatoes and add them to the bowl with the salt cod. Working by hand, blend the potatoes until they are broken up.
  4. Add 2 to 2-1/2 cups bread crumbs, cream, eggs, sautéed onion, lemon zest, lemon juice, dill, and black pepper and continue to mix until evenly blended. Place the flour, egg wash, and remaining bread crumbs in separate shallow bowls.
  5. Make a test portion. Shape the salt cod mixture into a small cake. Dredge the cake in the flour, shaking off the excess. Then dredge the cake in the egg wash, letting the excess drip back into the bowl. Finally, coat the cake with the bread crumbs.
  6. In a skillet, heat the remaining olive oil over medium-high heat until it reaches 375 F. Add the cake and fry for about 2 minutes on the first side until well browned, adjusting the heat if necessary. Turn the cake and cook on the other side for 2 to 3 minutes. Drain the cake briefly on paper towels.
  7. Taste the cake and check it for consistency. If the consistency seems too loose, add more bread crumbs to the fritter mixture. Adjust the seasoning as necessary with pepper, lemon juice, or dill.
  8. Shape and cook the remaining cod mixture in the same manner as the test portion. Keep the fritters warm in a 250 F oven until ready to serve. Serve with the aioli for dipping.
