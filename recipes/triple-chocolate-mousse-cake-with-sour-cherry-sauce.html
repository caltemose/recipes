Triple Chocolate Mousse Cake with Sour Cherry Sauce

CIA

Ingredients

  • 10 unit(s) egg yolks
  • 1 cup(s) sugar
  • 7 unit(s) egg white(s)
  • 0.67 cup(s) sugar
  • 1 cup(s) cocoa powder unsweetened, siftted
  • 2.25 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 1.5 ounce(s) semi-sweet chocolate chopped
  • 1.5 ounce(s) milk chocolate chopped
  • 1.5 ounce(s) white chocolate chopped
  • 0.33 unit(s) vanilla bean scraped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) plain gelatin
  • 1 tablespoon(s) rum dark rum
  • 0.25 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 2 unit(s) egg yolks
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) sugar

Directions

  1. One day before serving, preheat the oven to 350 F. Line a 10-inch round cake pan with parchment paper and spray it with vegetable oil spray.
  2. With the whip attachment of an electric mixer, whip the egg yolks and sugar on high speed until the mixture is very full in volume and recedes slightly from the highest point that it reached in the bowl.
  3. In a clean, oil-free bowl and with a clean whip, whip the egg whites until foamy. Add the sugar and continue to whip until medium-stiff peaks are formed.
  4. With a rubber spatula, gently fold the egg white mixture into the yolk mixture until incorporated. Then, fold in the sifted cocoa powder. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for approximately 15 to 18 minutes. Cool completely on a rack. Wrap the cooled cake with plastic wrap.
  5. To prepare the mousse layers, pour 1-1/2 cups of the heavy cream into a bowl and whip until soft peaks form. Reserve in the refrigerator.
  6. Place each of the chocolates in a separate bowl. In a saucepan, bring 1/4 cup of the heavy cream to a boil with the scraped vanilla bean. Remove and discard the vanilla bean and divide the hot cream among the bowls with the chocolate and stir each mixture until smooth. Reserve each mixture at room temperature.
  7. In the top of a double boiler, combine the gelatin and the rum or other liqueur. Place over a simmering water until the gelatin reaches approximately 90 F. Let cool.
  8. In another saucepan, bring the remaining 1/4 cup of heavy cream to a boil. In a stainless-steel bowl, beat the egg yolks with the sugar. While whisking, ladle a small amount of the hot cream into the yolks. Then, return the warm yolk mixture to the cream mixture on the stove and cook, stirring, until it coats the back of a metal spoon. Cool the mixture to room temperature.
  9. Add the gelatin mixture to the hot cream and egg yolk mixture and stir until the gelatin is dissolved. Then divide the mixture among the 3 types of chocolate and blend well.
  10. Place the flourless chocolate cake in a clean, lightly oiled springform pan and brush with the dark rum. Fold one-third of the reserved whipped cream into the dark chocolate mousse and spread it in an even layer over the flourless cake. Chill the cake in the freezer for 15 to 20 minutes. Fold one-third of the whipped cream into the milk chocolate mousse and spread it in an even layer over the dark chocolate mousse. Chill in the freezer for 15 to 20 minutes. Fold the remaining cream into the white chocolate mousse and spread it in an even layer over the milk chocolate mousse. Chill the cake in the refrigerator until the mousses are set, at least 3 hours.
  11. To serve, remove the sides of the springform pan. Dip a sharp knife with a long, thin blade into very hot water. Wipe the blade dry and make a cut. For the neatest slices, repeat this process for each separate slice that is made. Spoon a small amount of cherry sauce onto the plates around the cake servings and garnish with shaved chocolate.
