Marinated Berries

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 cup(s) sugar
  • 0.5 cup(s) water plus 2 T
  • 6 tablespoon(s) corn syrup
  • 3 tablespoon(s) cassis syrup
  • 3 tablespoon(s) Grand Marnier
  • 3 tablespoon(s) grenadine syrup
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 18 ounce(s) berries assorted, mixed

Directions

  1. In a saucepan, combine the sugar, water, corn syrup, cassis, Grand Marnier, grenadine, and lemon juice. Heat the mixture until the sugar is dissolved. Let cool. Place the berries in a bowl and add the marinade. Let them stand for at least 1 hour before serving, stirring occasionally.
