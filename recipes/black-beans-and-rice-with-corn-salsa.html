Black Beans and Rice with Corn Salsa

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) black beans dried
  • 1 unit(s) yellow onion(s) large, chopped
  • 1 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) large, seeded and cut into 1/2-inch dice
  • 0.25 cup(s) olive oil
  • 6 clove(s) garlic finely chopped
  • 0.33 cup(s) parsley chopped
  • 0.75 cup(s) cilantro chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon(s) ground cumin
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) oregano dried
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt plus salt to taste
  • 2 cup(s) water
  • 1 cup(s) basmati rice
  • 0.75 cup(s) dry white wine
  • 3 cup(s) corn salsa

Directions

  1. Pick over the black beans and discard any misshapen beans or stones. Rinse the beans and drain. Place in a bowl, add plenty of water to cover, and let soak for 3 hours.
  2. Drain the beans and place in a saucepan with the yellow onion, bell pepper, and water to cover by 2 inches. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer, uncovered, until the beans are tender, about 1 hour. Remove from the heat and reserve the beans in their cooking liquid.
  3. <ol class="RecipeDirections-List"><li class="RecipeDirections-Step" data-step-id="6878e06632ecade4c7b8b916">In a large frying pan over medium-low heat, warm the olive oil. Add the garlic, parsley, cilantro, brown sugar, cumin, oregano, 1-1/2 t salt, and pepper to taste. Saute, stirring occasionally, until the garlic is golden, about 10 minutes.</li></ol>
  4. In a&nbsp;heavy saucepan, bring the water and the remaining 1/2 t salt to a boil. Add the rice, stir once, then cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook for 20 minutes. After 20 minutes, uncover and check to see if the rice is tender and the water is absorbed. If not, recover and cook for a few minutes longer.
  5. Meanwhile, add the white wine to the garlic mixture and simmer over high heat until the wine is reduced by one-fourth, about 5 minutes. Reduce the heat to medium, add the beans and their cooking liquid and simmer, uncovered, until the liquid has evaporated, about 15 minutes.
  6. Spoon the rice into individual bowls. Top with the beans and the salsa. Serve at once.
