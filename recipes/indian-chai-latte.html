Indian Chai Latte

Rumya Khumar

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoon(s) black tea
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ginger grated
  • 2 unit(s) cardamom
  • 0.25 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s)
  • 2 unit(s) cloves
  • 3 unit(s) peppercorns
  • 0.5 unit(s) star anise
  • 0.5 cup(s) milk
  • 1.5 cup(s) water
  • 1 to taste sugar

Directions

  1. Lightly crush the whole spices and add to one cup of water with the loose tea. Bring the water just to a boil, then turn off the heat and let steep for 10 minutes. (Boiling the tea will make it bitter, so just bring it to a boil.)&nbsp;
  2. Add milk and bring to a boil again.
  3. &nbsp;Strain using a tea strainer. Add sugar to taste.
