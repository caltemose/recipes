New England Clam Chowder

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 ounce(s) salt pork minced
  • 1.25 cup(s) onion(s) chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) all-purpose flour
  • 3.5 cup(s) clam broth
  • 29 ounce(s) clams shucked, including their liquid (about 3-1/2 C)
  • 2 unit(s) potato peeled and diced
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) Worcestershire sauce
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 1 to taste Tabasco sauce
  • 2 cup(s) half and half
  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter cut into 8 cubes, for garnish

Directions

  1. In a large, heavy pot, cook the salt pork over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes or until most of the fat has been melted. Add the onion and cook, stirring, for about 5 to 8 minutes or until tender but not browned. Add the flour and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring constantly. Gradually whisk in the clam broth. Cook the soup, stirring occasionally, until thickened and smooth, about 10 minutes.
  2. Add the clams and potatoes to the soup and simmer until the potatoes are tender, about 20 minutes. Season with the Worcestershire sauce, white pepper, and Tabasco. Add the half-and-half and season with salt if necessary.
  3. Serve with chowder crackers. If desired, garnish each cup of chowder with a cube of fresh butter.
