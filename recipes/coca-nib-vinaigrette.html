Coca Nib Vinaigrette

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) cocoa nibs
  • 4 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 3 tablespoon(s) balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) shallot(s) minced
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Pulse the cocoa nibs in a spice grinder 4 or 5 times until finely ground.
  2. Combine the ground cocoa nibs and the olive oil in a 1-quart saucepan and heat on low for 2 to 3 minutes until warm and fragrant.
  3. Add the vinegar, shallot, salt, and red pepper flake, and let sit for about 1 hour until cooled to room temperature.
  4. Transfer to a jar, twist on the lid, and shake like crazy to combine.
  5. Salad suggestion #1: butter lettuce, avocado, orange slices, dried cherries, bittersweet chocolate shavings, cocoa nibs
  6. Salad suggestion #2: endive or radicchio, green grapes, crumbled bacon, blue cheese, bittersweet chocolate shavings, cocoa nibs
  7. Salad suggestion #3: spring mix, strawberries, crumbled goat cheese, bittersweet chocolate shaving, cocoa nibs
