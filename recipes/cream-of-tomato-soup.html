Cream of Tomato Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 3.5 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 unit(s) onion(s) finely chopped
  • 2 stalk(s) celery finely chopped
  • 2 unit(s) carrot(s) finely diced
  • 2 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 2.25 pound(s) roma tomato(es) quartered, roasted, and coarsely chopped
  • 1.25 pound(s) roma tomato(es) peeled and finely chopped
  • 6 unit(s) sun-dried tomatoes finely chopped
  • 1 quart(s) vegetable stock hot
  • 2 tablespoon(s) heavy cream 2-3 T

Directions

  1. Heat the butter and olive oil in a heavy saucepan over medium heat. Add the onions and saute for 8-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until very soft but not colored. Next, add the celery and carrots, and continue cooking gently without burning for another 10 minutes, stirring from time to time. Add the garlic and saute for another 2 minutes, stirring.
  2. Mix together the roasted plum tomatoes, fresh tomatoes, and sun-dried tomatoes. Add them into the pan with any juices, and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes to allow the flavors to combine. If the sauce looks too thick or starts sticking to the bottom of the pan, add a little of the hot vegetable stock. Pour in the remaining vegetable stock and simmer the soup for 15-20 minutes.
  3. Blend the soup to a smooth puree using a food processor. Pass through a sieve or mouli into a clean pan, unless you prefer to make a peasant-style soup. Add the heavy cream a teaspoon at a time until you are happy with the taste and texture. Season with salt and pepper, reheat very gently if needed, and serve.
