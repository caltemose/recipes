Chilled Melon and White Wine Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) cantaloupe or other melon, 2-1/4 lb
  • 0.67 cup(s) sugar
  • 1 cup(s) dry white wine
  • 1 to taste lemon juice
  • 0.67 cup(s) creme fraiche

Directions

  1. Discard the seeds and scoop the flesh from the melon. Ideally you should have 1-1/2 lb weight, but don’t worry if you have more or less. The other quantities can be adjusted to your taste and this will depend more on the strength of the melon’s flavor than on its bulk.
  2. Dissolve the sugar in 2-1/2 cups of water over low heat, then simmer for 4-5 minutes. Remove from the heat and let cool completely.
  3. In a food processor or blender, process the melon with the wine until smooth, then gradually add the cooled simple syrup. Stop when the mixture tastes just a little sweeter than you would ideally like it. Now mix in a few good squeezes of lemon juice and the creme fraiche. Serve chilled with tiny almond cookies or meringues.
