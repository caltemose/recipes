Shakshuka

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) seeded and chopped
  • 0.5 unit(s) yellow onion(s) large, finely chopped
  • 2 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground coriander
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) paprika sweet
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) paprika hot
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes ground
  • 1 can(s) tomato(es) 28 oz can plus juice, or 4 tomatoes plus 1/2 C tomato sauce
  • 6 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 1 unit(s) parsley chopped, for garnish
  • 1 unit(s) basil chopped, for garnish
  • 1 unit(s) feta cheese for garnish

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet. Add the onion, pepper, garlic, spices, and salt and pepper to taste. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables have softened, about 5 minutes.
  2. Chop the canned tomatoes. Add tomatoes and their juice to the skillet. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes. Uncover and cook a bit longer to reduce to a thick sauce. Taste and adjust seasoning to your liking.
  3. Using a large spoon, make 4 indentations in the sauce. Gently crack an egg into each indentation.
  4. Reduce the heat, cover the skillet, and cook on low until the whites of the eggs are set, 2-3 minutes.
  5. Spoon the tomato sauce into bowls and place two eggs on top in each bowl. Garnish with parsley and/or basil and crumbled feta. Serve with crusty bread.
