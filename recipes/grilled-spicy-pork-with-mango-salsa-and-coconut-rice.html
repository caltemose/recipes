Grilled Spicy Pork with Mango Salsa and Coconut Rice

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup(s) allspice ground
  • 1 teaspoon(s) ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon(s) nutmeg
  • 5 unit(s) green onion roughly chopped
  • 0.5 unit(s) onion(s) roughly chopped
  • 1 unit(s) scotch bonnet pepper(s) stemmed and seeded
  • 2 tablespoon(s) rum dark rum
  • 0.25 cup(s) pineapple juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon(s) salt
  • 2 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 6 unit(s) pork tenderloin(s)
  • 1 unit(s) vegetable oil
  • 5 unit(s) chayote squash
  • 1 pound(s) carrot(s) cut into julienne
  • 0.25 cup(s) butter
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 1 unit(s) coconut rice
  • 1 unit(s) mango salsa

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients for the jerk paste in a blender or food processor and process until smooth. You should have a thick but moist paste. Set aside.
  2. Trim the pork of excess fat and sinew. In a skillet, heat a small amount of vegetable oil over high heat. Add the pork tenderloins and cook quickly until seared on all sides. Let cool. When the pork is cool enough to handle, rub it well with the jerk paste. Set aside.
  3. Place the whole chayotes in boiling salted water and cook them until tender. Cool the squash in ice water and remove the peels. Remove the seeds and cut the chayotes into julienne.
  4. Prepare a medium-hot charcoal fire or preheat the grill to medium-high. Place the pork on the grill and cook, turning occasionally, for 12 to 15 minutes until the internal temperature is 170 F. Remove the pork from the grill and set aside.
  5. In a skillet, heat the butter over medium-high heat. Add the blanched chayotes and the carrots and saute until the carrots are tender and the chayotes are heated through. Add the chopped cilantro and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Cut the pork into serving portions. Divide the chayote-carrot mixture among twelve serving plates and top with the pork. Accompany the pork with coconut rice and top with mango salsa.
