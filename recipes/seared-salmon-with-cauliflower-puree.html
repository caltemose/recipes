Seared Salmon with Cauliflower Puree

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 head(s) cauliflower chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) butter cut into pieces
  • 0.33 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 6 unit(s) salmon 5-oz fillets
  • 1 unit(s) olive oil for brushing

Directions

  1. Steam the cauliflower in a covered pan over boiling water until tender, about 8 to 10 minutes. In a food processor or blender, puree the hot cauliflower. Pour into a bowl and stir in a few pieces of butter and the cream until blended. Set aside and keep warm.
  2. Brush the salmon fillets with the oil and season with salt and pepper. Heat a large nonstick saucepan or skillet over high heat until smoking and saute the salmon for 2 to 3 minutes. Turn the fish over and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes or to the desired doneness. Serve at once with the warm puree.
