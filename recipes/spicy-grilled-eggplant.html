Spicy Grilled Eggplant

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 9 unit(s) eggplant(s) Asian, or 2 globe eggplants, about 2 lb total
  • 6 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 3 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. Cut the eggplants crosswise into slices 1/4 inch thick. If you are using globe eggplants, place the slices in a colander and salt them liberally to draw out the moisture. Let stand for 30 minutes. Rinse with water and pat dry with paper towels. If you are using slender eggplants, there is no need to salt them.
  2. Brush the eggplant slices with 4 tablespoons of the olive oil. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat an oven to 400 F (200 F).
  4. Oil a baking sheet and arrange the eggplant slices on it in a single layer. Bake on the top rack of the oven, turning occasionally, until golden on both sides, about 15 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, in a small bowl, stir together the garlic, the remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil, and the vinegar.
  6. Place the eggplant on a serving platter and drizzle the garlic-oil mixture over the top. Sprinkle with the red pepper flakes and parsley and serve.
