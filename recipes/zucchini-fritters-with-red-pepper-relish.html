Zucchini Fritters with Red Pepper Relish

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 6 unit(s) zucchini small, 1-1/4 lb total, trimmed
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt plus salt to taste
  • 2 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) roasted, peeled, seeded, and finely chopped
  • 1 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped
  • 1 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 0.67 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 1 unit(s) vegetable oil for deep frying

Directions

  1. Using the large holes on a hand-held shredder or a food processor fitted with the shredding blade, shred the zucchini. Place in a colander set over a bowl and sprinkle with the 1/2 t salt to draw out the moisture. Let stand for 1 hour.
  2. In a bowl, combine the bell peppers, tomato, garlic, cumin, cayenne, olive oil, 1 T lemon juice, and parsley. Stir to mix well. Season the relish to taste with salt and pepper and additional lemon juice if needed. Set aside.
  3. Remove the zucchini from the colander and pat dry with paper towels. In a bowl, lightly beat the egg. Sift the flour over the egg and stir to mix well. Stir the zucchini into the egg mixture. Season to taste with pepper and mix well.
  4. In a deep, heavy saucepan, pour in the canola oil to a depth of 1 inch. Heat to 375 F on a deep-fat frying thermometer or until a small drop of the zucchini mixture sizzles immediately upon contact with the oil.
  5. When the oil is hot, drop in the zucchini mixture by tablespoonfuls, a few at a time. Do not crowd the pan. Fry, turning once, until golden brown on both sides, 1-2 minutes total. Using a slotted spoon, transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and keep warm until all are cooked. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  6. Transfer the fritters to a warmed platter or individual plates and serve immediately. Pass the pepper relish at the table.
