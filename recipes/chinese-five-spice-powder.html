Chinese Five-Spice Powder

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 15 unit(s) star anise
  • 1 stick(s) cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoon(s) fennel seeds
  • 1 tablespoon(s) Sichuan peppercorns
  • 15 unit(s) cloves

Directions

  1. Crush the anise and cinnamon roughly by wrapping them in a tea towel and smacking them with a rolling pin. Then transfer everything to a spice grinder and pulse to a powder.
  2. Store in an airtight container for up to 90 days.
