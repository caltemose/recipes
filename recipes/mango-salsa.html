Mango Salsa

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) mango 10 ounces, cut into small dice
  • 0.5 unit(s) red onion(s) medium, cut into small dice
  • 1 teaspoon(s) jalapeño pepper(s) minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) lime zest
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 tablespoon(s) basil chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) lime zest finely chopped

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a food processor and pulse until the desired consistency is achieved. For a chunkier consistency, mix the ingredients by hand. Let the mixture stand for 1 hour to blend the flavors.
