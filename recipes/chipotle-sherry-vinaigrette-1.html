Chipotle-Sherry Vinaigrette #1

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.75 cup(s) dry sherry vinegar plus 1 T
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 4 unit(s) chipotle chilies in adobo minced
  • 2 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) maple syrup or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon(s) salt or to taste
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) black pepper or to taste
  • 3 cup(s) olive oil
  • 0.5 cup(s) fresh herbs chives, parsley, tarragon, chervil, chopped

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the vinegar, lime, chipotles, shallots, garlic, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. While whisking, slowly drizzle in the oil. Just before serving, mix in the herbs. Taste the dressing and adjust with maple syrup, salt, and pepper if necessary.
