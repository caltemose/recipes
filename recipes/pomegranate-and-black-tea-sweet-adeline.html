Pomegranate and Black Tea (Sweet Adeline)

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup(s) pomegranate juice
  • 1 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s) or 1 T cinnamon simple syrup
  • 1 unit(s) black tea Earl Gray
  • 0.75 cup(s) water

Directions

  1. For tea, add pomegranate juice and cinnamon syrup to mug. Add hot water and teabag. Steep for 3-5 minutes.
