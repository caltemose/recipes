Carrot and Orange Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) leek(s) sliced
  • 1.1 pound(s) carrot(s) sliced
  • 1 unit(s) potato about 4 oz, chopped
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground coriander
  • 1 pinch ground cumin
  • 1.25 cup(s) orange juice
  • 2 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 2 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped, for garnish
  • 1 to taste yogurt for garnish

Directions

  1. Put the oil, leek, and carrots in a large saucepan and cook over low heat for 5 minutes, stirring frequently, or until the leek has softened. Add the potato, coriander, and cumin, then pour in the orange juice and stock. Add the bay leaf and stir occasionally.
  2. Increase the heat, bring the soup to a boil, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 40 minutes or until the vegetables are very tender.
  3. Let the soup cool slightly, then transfer to a blender and process until smooth, working in batches if necessary.
  4. Return to the saucepan and add a little extra stock or water if the soup is too thick. Bring back to a simmer, then transfer to heated serving bowls and sprinkle with chopped cilantro. Add a swirl of cream or a spoonful of plain yogurt.
