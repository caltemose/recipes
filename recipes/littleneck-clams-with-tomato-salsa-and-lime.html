Littleneck Clams with Tomato Salsa and Lime

CIA

Ingredients

  • 3 bottle(s) lager
  • 72 unit(s) clams littleneck clams, scrubbed
  • 6 tablespoon(s) lime flesh diced
  • 1 to taste cilantro to garnish
  • 2 unit(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) red onion(s) finely diced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 0.5 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) seeded and minced

Directions

  1. To make the salsa: Combine all the ingredients and let sit at room temperature for at least 4 hours and up to overnight.
  2. Pour 2 bottles of the beer into a large pot. Bring to a boil, add the clams, cover and steam until the clams open, about 5 to 7 minutes.
  3. Pour 1/4 cup of the remaining beer in the bottom of each bowl. Transfer the steamed clams to the bowls (discard any that have not opened). Spoon 3 teaspoons of the salsa and 1 tablespoon of the diced lime over each serving. Garnish with cilantro.
