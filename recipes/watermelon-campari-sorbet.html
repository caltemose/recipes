Watermelon Campari Sorbet

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound(s) watermelon cubed
  • 9 ounce(s) sugar
  • 3 tablespoon(s) Campari
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lime zest
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt

Directions

  1. Pure the watermelon in a blender or food processor. Add the sugar, Campari, lime zest, lime juice, and salt, and process for another 30 seconds. Transfer to an airtight container and chill for 1 hour in the refrigerator.
  2. Churn in an ice cream maker, then return to the airtight container for 3 to 4 hours to harden.
