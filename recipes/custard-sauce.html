Custard Sauce

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) milk
  • 2 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 0.5 unit(s) vanilla bean split lengthwise or 1 t vanilla extract
  • 1 cup(s) sugar
  • 8 unit(s) egg yolks

Directions

  1. In a large, heavy saucepan, combine the milk, cream, vanilla bean or extract, and 1/2 cup of the sugar. Mix well and bring to a simmer over medium heat.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine the remaining 1/2 cup sugar with the egg yolks. Whisk until thoroughly combined. Whisk about 1/2 cup of the hot milk mixture into the egg yolks. Gradually whisk in 1 more cup. Whisk the yolk mixture into the hot milk. Gently cook the sauce over low heat until it is thick enough to coat a spoon.
  3. Strain the sauce through a fine-meshed sieve into a bowl set over ice. Stir the sauce as it cools.
