Streusel Topping

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup(s) butter at room temperature
  • 0.5 cup(s) brown sugar packed
  • 0.5 cup(s) wheat bran
  • 1 cup(s) rolled oats
  • 0.5 cup(s) wheat germ
  • 0.5 cup(s) walnuts chopped
  • 0.25 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 0.12 teaspoon(s) salt

Directions

  1. In a medium bowl, blend all the ingredients together. Use now or cover and refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Yield

3.5 cups
