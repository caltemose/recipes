Dilled Potato Croquettes

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 1.75 pound(s) potato russet, well scrubbed
  • 2 unit(s) egg yolks
  • 4 unit(s) green onion including the green tops, finely chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) dill chopped, plus dill sprigs to garnish
  • 0.75 cup(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 2 teaspoon(s) white wine vinegar
  • 1 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 1 unit(s) vegetable oil for deep-frying
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 2.5 cup(s) bread crumbs fine, dried

Directions

  1. Fill a large saucepan three-fourths full with water, salt it lightly, and bring to a boil. Add the potatoes and cook until tender when pierced with a fork, 25-35 minutes. Drain and let cool.
  2. Peel the potatoes and then puree them by passing them through a food mill fitted with the fine disk placed over a large bowl, or a potato ricer placed over a large bowl. Add the egg yolks, green onions, chopped dill, parmesan, vinegar, and salt and pepper to taste. Mix well. Cover and refrigerate for 1 hour.
  3. Spread the flour on a plate. Using floured hands, shape the potato mixture into 24 ovals that are 1-1/2 inches long and 3/4 inch wide. As each oval is formed, roll it in the flour to coat evenly and then tap off the excess. Place the croquettes well spaced in a single layer on a baking sheet.
  4. Preheat an oven to 400 F (200 C).
  5. In a deep, heavy frying pan, pour in the canola oil to a depth of 1/2 inch. Heat to 375 F on a deep-fat frying thermometer or until a small drop of the potato mixture sizzles upon contact with the oil.
  6. Meanwhile, in a bowl, lightly beat the eggs. Place the bread crumbs in another bowl. Dip the croquettes first in the eggs, then in the crumbs, coating evenly. As each croquette is coated, return it to the baking sheet.
  7. Carefully slip the croquettes into the hot oil a few at a time and fry, turning occasionally, until golden, 2-3 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer to paper towels to drain briefly, then transfer to a platter and keep warm while you cook the remaining croquettes.
  8. Garnish with dill sprigs and serve at once.
