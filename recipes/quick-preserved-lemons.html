Quick Preserved Lemons

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 4 unit(s) lemon(s) medium, scrubbed and dried
  • 0.25 cup(s) salt coarse sea salt
  • 1 unit(s) lemon(s) juiced

Directions

  1. Remove top and tail from each lemon.
  2. Slice each lemon into 8 wedges, removing any seeds.
  3. Layer sliced lemons in a wide-mouthed jar - sprinkling with salt at each layer.
  4. Top the jar with any remaining lemon juice, leaving about 1/2 inch of head space in the jar.
  5. Stash in the refrigerator for 4 days, then flip the bottle over and age another 4 days before sampling.&nbsp; The peel should be nice and soft.
  6. This can be used immediately or kept in the fridge for up to 3 months.
