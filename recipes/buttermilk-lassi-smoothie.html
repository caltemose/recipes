Buttermilk Lassi Smoothie

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 4 unit(s) frozen buttermilk lassi cubes
  • 0.5 unit(s) cantaloupe cubed
  • 1 unit(s) peach peeled and pitted, or 1 C frozen peach slices
  • 0.5 cup(s) yogurt
  • 1 unit(s) ginger 1-inch-long piece
  • 0.5 cup(s) almond milk
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) turmeric

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender. Puree until smooth. Serve immediately.
