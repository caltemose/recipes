Corn-and-Jalapeno Pancakes with Tomato Salsa

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) corn from 3 ears
  • 5 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) seeded and minced
  • 2 clove(s) garlic finely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) seeded and finely diced
  • 6 unit(s) green onion white part and 2 inches of the green tops, thinly sliced
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 1.25 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 0.5 cup(s) corn meal
  • 1 teaspoon(s) baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon(s) salt plus salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 1.5 cup(s) milk
  • 2 cup(s) fresh salsa
  • 0.33 cup(s) vegetable oil
  • 1.25 cup(s) sour cream

Directions

  1. Fill a saucepan three-fourths full with water, salt it lightly, and bring to a boil. Add the corn kernels and cook for 1 minute. Drain, place in a large bowl, and let cool. Add the jalapeño, garlic, bell pepper, and green onions. Mix well and set aside.
  2. In a food processor fitted with the metal blade, combine the eggs, flour, cornmeal, baking powder, 1 t salt, and the lime juice. Pulse a few times to mix. Add the milk and pulse a few more times to form a smooth batter. Add to the corn mixture and stir to mix. Season to taste with more salt, if needed, and pepper. Let stand at room temperature for 30 minutes.
  3. To make the tomato salsa, in a bowl, stir together the tomatoes, red onion, jalapeño, lime juice, cilantro, and salt and pepper to taste. Set aside.
  4. In a large frying pan over medium heat, warm 2 tablespoons of the oil. Working in batches, spoon the batter into the pan to form pancakes 3 inches in diameter. Do not crowd the pan. Cook, turning once, until golden brown on both sides, 5-6 minutes total. Using a slotted spatula, transfer to paper towels to drain. Repeat with the remaining batter, adding oil as needed to prevent sticking.
  5. Place the pancakes on a platter or individual plates and top with the sour cream and salsa. Serve at once.
