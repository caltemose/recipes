Grilled Shrimp and Pineapple with Adobo de Achiote

CIA

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoon(s) achiote seeds
  • 1 tablespoon(s) allspice berries
  • 2 teaspoon(s) peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon(s) oregano Mexican
  • 2 tablespoon(s) apple cider vinegar
  • 3 unit(s) lime(s) juiced
  • 6 clove(s) garlic roughly chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) salt
  • 1 cup(s) vegetable oil or olive oil
  • 72 unit(s) jumbo shrimp peeled, deveined, tail on
  • 1 unit(s) pineapple cut into 72 chunks
  • 6 unit(s) red onion(s) cut into 48 wedges
  • 1 unit(s) salsa cruda
  • 1 unit(s) cilantro leaves for garnish

Directions

  1. Place the achiote seeds, allspice, peppercorns, oregano, vinegar, lime juice, garlic, salt, and oil in a blender and puree until a very smooth paste forms. Pour the paste into a shallow pan.
  2. On each of 24 skewers, thread 3 shrimp, 3 pineapple chunks, and 2 red onion wedges, alternating ingredients. As you assemble the skewers, place them in the pan with the achiote paste, turning the skewers to coat them evenly. Let the skewers marinate for at least 1 hour before cooking.
  3. Fifteen minutes before you're ready to cook, preheat the grill to medium and brush the grates to clean them. Place the skewers on the grill and cook for about 2 to 3 minutes per side, until the shrimp are just cooked through (take care not to overcook the shrimp).
  4. Place the skewers on a serving platter and serve hot or at room temperature with guacamole and salsa cruda. Garnish the platter with cilantro.
