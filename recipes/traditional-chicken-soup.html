Traditional Chicken Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) chicken whole small to medium chicken
  • 1 unit(s) carrot(s) large, chopped
  • 1 unit(s) leek(s) cut into segments
  • 3 clove(s) garlic unpeeled
  • 0.75 ounce(s) mushrooms dried
  • 3 stalk(s) celery chopped
  • 2 sprig(s) parsley 2-3 sprigs
  • 2 sprig(s) thyme 2-3 sprigs
  • 3.5 tablespoon(s) white rice short grain
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 0.25 ounce(s) butter
  • 0.5 unit(s) red onion(s) large, finely chopped
  • 2 clove(s) garlic crushed
  • 9 ounce(s) mushrooms button, thinly sliced
  • 4 tablespoon(s) half and half
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley finely chopped
  • 2 teaspoon(s) lemon zest finely grated

Directions

  1. For the stock, place the chicken in a large saucepan over low heat. Cover with water and bring slowly to a boil, skimming the surface as needed. Add the carrot, leek, garlic, dried mushrooms, celery, and herbs. Season lightly. Bring back to a simmer, partially cover, and cook for 1 hour.
  2. Remove from the heat. Once cool, lift out the chicken, strip the meat from the carcass, and reserve. Return the carcass to the pan and boil for 1 hour. Let cool, strain through a sieve into a bowl, and chill the stock.
  3. Soak the rice in cold water for 30 minutes. Rinse and drain. Skim the fat from the chilled stock. You’ll need 3-1/4 cups of stock for the soup recipe, but you can add boiling water if needed.
  4. Put the oil and butter in a deep saucepan over medium heat. Add the vegetables and cook for 3-5 minutes. Add the rice and stir for 2 minutes. Add the stock and cook for 15-20 minutes. Shred about 7 oz of the reserved chicken and add to the soup. Taste, season if necessary, and return to a simmer. Stir in the half and half, parsley, and lemon zest and serve very hot.
