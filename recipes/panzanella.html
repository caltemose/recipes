Panzanella

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf bread country-style, small loaf, a few days old
  • 1 cup(s) water
  • 6 unit(s) tomato(es) seeded and cut into 1/2-inch dice
  • 1 unit(s) red onion(s) small, thinly sliced
  • 1 unit(s) cucumber(s) peeled, halved lengthwise, seeded, and cut into 1/2-inch dice
  • 2 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 3 tablespoon(s) capers drained
  • 0.5 cup(s) basil lightly packed, carefully washed, well dried, torn into pieces
  • 1 cup(s) balsamic and red wine vinegar dressing

Directions

  1. Cut the bread crosswise into slices 1 inch thick. Place in a large shallow container and pour the water evenly over the slices. Let stand for 1 minute.
  2. Carefully squeeze the bread between your hands until dry. Tear the bread into rough 1-inch pieces and spread out on paper towels to absorb any excess moisture for 10 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, combine the tomatoes, onion, cucumber, garlic, capers, basil, and bread. Toss together to mix well.
  4. Add the dressing and toss again until evenly distributed. Cover and let rest in the refrigerator for 1 hour.
  5. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Transfer to a platter or individual plates and serve.
