Mexican Sweet Corn Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) red onion(s) finely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) seeded and finely chopped
  • 1 clove(s) garlic crushed
  • 1 teaspoon(s) fennel seeds
  • 1 teaspoon(s) thyme fresh
  • 14 ounce(s) corn drained and rinsed if canned
  • 0.5 cup(s) half and half
  • 2.5 tablespoon(s) olive oil chili-flavored, to serve

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a large saute pan over medium heat, add the onion, bell pepper (reserve a scant tablespoon to finish), garlic, fennel seeds, and thyme, and stir-fry for 3 minutes. Add the sweet corn and season lightly. Continue stirring and cooking for 2 minutes, then pour in 2 cups hot water, stir, and bring to a simmer. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes or until the vegetables are tender and cooked through. Stir in the half and half and let cool for a few minutes.
  2. Process the soup until smooth in a blender. You may need to do this in batches. Return to the pan and reheat gently, stirring occasionally. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Ladle into bowls and add the reserved red pepper. Drizzle a little chili-flavored olive oil over the top. Serve at once.
  3. To make the flavored olive oil, stir a little bit of piquant pimenton, harissa, or smoked paprika into fruity olive oil.
