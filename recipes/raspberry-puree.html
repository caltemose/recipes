Raspberry Puree

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup(s) sugar
  • 0.25 cup(s) red wine burgundy
  • 2.5 cup(s) raspberries pureed and strained

Directions

  1. In a large saucepan, combine all the ingredients. Bring to a simmer and cook for 3 minutes. Strain through a fine-meshed sieve. Cover and refrigerate until needed.
