Marinated Strawberries

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) strawberries quartered
  • 2 tablespoon(s) sugar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) Grand Marnier
  • 2 tablespoon(s) orange juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) honey
  • 0.12 teaspoon(s) vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon(s) mint finely chopped leaves

Directions

  1. In a bowl, combine all of the ingredients and toss gently until evenly blended. Refrigerate the berries for 1 hour before serving, stirring occasionally.
