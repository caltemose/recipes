Pumpkin Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) pumpkin small, about 1 lb 10 oz, peeled and cubed
  • 2 unit(s) potato medium, peeled and cubed
  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2.5 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 2 unit(s) tomato(es) chopped
  • 4 leave(s) sage
  • 1 unit(s) olive oil
  • 18 leave(s) sage large

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and cook the pumpkin and potatoes for 5 minutes. Add the stock, chopped tomatoes, and the 4 sage leaves. Cover and simmer for about 10 minutes, until the vegetables are soft.
  2. Using a blender, process the soup until smooth, then gently reheat. Season with salt and pepper, to taste.
  3. For the garnish, heat a little oil in a frying pan and, once hot, fry the large sage leaves until crisp. Scatter the crispy leaves over the soup and serve immediately.
