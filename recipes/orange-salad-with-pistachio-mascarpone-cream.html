Orange Salad with Pistachio-Mascarpone Cream

Fran's Olive Oil Cookbook

Ingredients

  • 4 unit(s) orange(s)
  • 14 ounce(s) mascarpone
  • 4 ounce(s) pistachio cream
  • 12 leave(s) basil
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 4 tablespoon(s) pistachios chopped

Directions

  1. Stir the mascarpone and the pistachio cream until smooth and keep it cool in the fridge for about 30 minutes.
  2. Peel and fillet the oranges.
  3. Arrange the orange pieces on 4 plates. Dollop the pistachio cream on top, sprinkle with chopped pistachios and several sea salt flakes and a little ground black pepper. Garnish with several basil leaves and drizzle with a splash of olive oil.
