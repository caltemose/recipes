Fresh Salsa

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) tomato(es) seeded and finely diced
  • 4 unit(s) tomatillo(s) husked and finely diced
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) finely diced
  • 0.25 cup(s) lime juice about 2 limes
  • 2 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) seeded and finely diced
  • 0.25 cup(s) cilantro minced

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients and let sit for 1 to 2 hours. Serve immediately, or cover and refrigerate for up to 3 days.
