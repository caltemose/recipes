Braised Artichokes with Lemons, Olive Oil, and Thyme

CIA

Ingredients

  • 4 unit(s) artichoke hearts large
  • 1 unit(s) lemon(s) juiced, reserve lemon halves after juicing
  • 0.75 cup(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 3 clove(s) garlic sliced
  • 0.25 bunch thyme
  • 8 unit(s) peppercorns
  • 0.5 cup(s) dry white wine
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. Cut the hearts into quarters. In a medium bowl, combine the artichokes, 2 tablespoons of the lemon juice, and cold water to cover. The artichokes may be left in this solution for up to 3 hours. Drain just before braising.
  2. In a medium saute pan or skillet over medium heat, heat the oil and saute the bay leaf, garlic, and thyme for 1 minute. Add the artichokes, lemon halves, and any remaining juice and saute, stirring, for 30 seconds. Season with salt and add the white wine. Cover and simmer gently until the artichokes are tender, about 20 minutes. Check often and add more wine or water as necessary to prevent browning.
  3. Add the parsley and serve warm or chilled, as a side dish or starter.
