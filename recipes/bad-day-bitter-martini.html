Bad Day Bitter Martini

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) ice cubes
  • 2.5 ounce(s) gin
  • 0.5 ounce(s) montenegro
  • 1 unit(s) grapefruit peel

Directions

  1. Wrap ice cubes in a clean towel and smack it against the counter a few times to crush. Fill a martini glass with 1 C of crushed ice. Add the remaining ice to a cocktail shaker.
  2. Add gin and Montenegro and stir until chilled, about 15 seconds. Discard the ice from the martini glass and strain the gin mixture into the glass. Garnish with grapefruit peel.
