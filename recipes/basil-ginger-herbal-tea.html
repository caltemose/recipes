Basil-Ginger Herbal Tea

The Indian Vegan Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) water
  • 2 teaspoon(s) ginger grated
  • 0.25 cup(s) basil small leaves
  • 1 to taste sugar

Directions

  1. Boil water with ginger and basil. Remove from heat and steep 3-5 minutes.
  2. Strain tea. Serve hot. Add sugar if desired.
