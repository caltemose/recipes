Moussaka

CIA

Ingredients

  • 3 unit(s) eggplant(s)
  • 0.33 cup(s) olive oil
  • 3 unit(s) onion(s) diced
  • 1.25 pound(s) ground lamb
  • 2 cup(s) canned tomatoes chopped, with juice
  • 3 clove(s) garlic
  • 4 unit(s) cloves
  • 1 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s) or 1/4 t ground cinnamon
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 1 pinch allspice
  • 0.5 cup(s) water
  • 2 tablespoon(s) tomato paste
  • 0.25 cup(s) red wine optional
  • 5 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 5 tablespoon(s) all-purpose flour
  • 2.5 cup(s) milk
  • 1 to taste nutmeg
  • 2 unit(s) egg yolks
  • 0.25 cup(s) bread crumbs optional
  • 0.5 cup(s) parmeggiano grated

Directions

  1. In a large nonstick or cast iron skillet, heat about 1 tablespoon of the olive oil. In batches, add the eggplant slices and saute for about 2 to 3 minutes on each side, adding more oil as necessary. Drain the eggplant on paper towels.
  2. Cut off the stems and tips from the eggplants and cut them into slices about 1/2-inch thick. Place the eggplant slices in a colander and sprinkle liberally with salt. Place the colander in a large bowl or in the sink to drain for about 45 minutes. Rinse the eggplant well and blot it dry with paper towels.
  3. In a large skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the onions and saute until translucent, about 15 to 20 minutes. Add the ground lamb and saute until it is crumbly and no pink remains.
  4. Add the tomatoes, garlic, cloves, cinnamon, bay leaf, allspice, salt, pepper, and water. Bring the mixture to a simmer and simmer for 45 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste and red wine, if desired, and simmer for another 10 minutes, until the sauce is very thick and well flavored.
  5. In a saucepan, heat the butter over medium heat. Add the flour and cook, stirring, for about 5 minutes. Gradually whisk in the milk, taking care to work out any lumps that form. Bring the milk to a full boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to low and simmer gently, stirring frequently, for 30 minutes or until the sauce has thickened.
  6. Remove the sauce from the heat, stir in the nutmeg, and season with salt and pepper. In a small bowl, whisk the egg yolks. Add a small amount of the hot sauce to the bowl with the yolks and blend well. Transfer the contents of the bowl to the pan, stir well and keep warm.
  7. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Scatter the bread crumbs, if using, in a deep, rectangular baking dish. Layer half of the eggplant slices over the bread crumbs. Pour in the meat sauce and spread it evenly. Layer the remaining eggplant over the meat sauce. Pour the béchamel (milk sauce) over the eggplant and spread it evenly. Sprinkle with the cheese.
  8. Place the moussaka in the oven and bake for about 1 hour or until the béchamel is thick and golden brown. Let the moussaka stand at room temperature for about 20 minutes before cutting into serving portions.
