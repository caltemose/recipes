Widow's Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) large, chopped
  • 1 unit(s) potato large, chopped
  • 6 ounce(s) cauliflower quartered florets
  • 6 ounce(s) carrot(s) sliced
  • 1 head(s) lettuce small, shredded
  • 6 ounce(s) peas fresh or frozen and thawed
  • 9 ounce(s) tomato(es) peeled and coarsely chopped, to 7 oz canned
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) tomato puree
  • 1 teaspoon(s) sugar
  • 1 teaspoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 6 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 9 ounce(s) ricotta or firm young goat cheese

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion until soft, but not colored. Add the potato, cauliflower, carrots, lettuce, and fresh peas, if using. Stir, then add the tomatoes, tomato puree, sugar, vinegar, salt, and pepper and enough water (or stock) to cover generously. Bring to a boil and simmer until the vegetables are almost tender. If using thawed frozen peas, add them now. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  2. Reduce the heat so that the surface of the water is barely simmering. Break the eggs one at a time onto a saucer or ramekin and slide them into the soup. Poach the eggs until the white has just set, about 8-10 minutes.
  3. Cut the cheese into 4-6 portions, according to the number of serving bowls, and place one in each bowl. Carefully place one egg in each bowl, and ladle the soup around the eggs and cheese before serving.
