Whole Wheat English Muffins

Ingredients

  • 2.25 cup(s) milk whole
  • 2 tablespoon(s) honey
  • 2.25 teaspoon(s) yeast
  • 1 unit(s) egg(s) large
  • 4 tablespoon(s) butter melted
  • 4.75 cup(s) whole wheat flour
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 unit(s) corn meal for dusting

Directions

  1. In a small saucepan, warm the milk and honey over low heat until it reaches 110 degrees F. The milk will be hot but not steaming, and not too hot to put your finger in it. Remove from heat, stir in the yeast, and set aside for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the flour and salt to a large bowl and mix well. Gradually pour the yeast mixture into the flour, a little at a time, mixing it into the flour. Dough will be slightly sticky.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured work surface for 8 to 10 minutes. Add more flour, little by little, if dough becomes too sticky while kneading. Don’t add too much flour and don’t force the dough to incorporate flour. In the end, the dough should feel slightly tacky when gently pressed with your fingertip.
  4. Form the dough into a ball and place it in a lightly oiled bowl. Roll the dough around so that all surfaces are oiled. Cover with plastic wrap and set in a warm place to rise for 45 minutes or until doubled in size.
  5. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper and generously dust the paper with cornmeal. Try to make the dusting as evenly as possible because it will all stick to the muffins. Set aside.
  6. Gently punch the dough down and turn it out onto a lightly floured surface. Gently knead the dough together into a ball and divide it in half. Divide each half into 8 equal-sized pieces. You should have 16 dough balls.
  7. Roll each piece into a ball and then flatten the ball into a disk approximately 1/2" thick. (If the dough is too sticky, lightly oil your hands to make it easier to handle the dough.) The muffins will puff up when baking, so don’t worry if they look too flat at this point.
  8. Place the disks on the prepared baking sheets. Sprinkle a little cornmeal over the tops. Cover with a lint-free towel and set in a draft-free place for 20 minutes (or longer).
  9. Preheat the oven to 325 F.
  10. Heat a frying pan over medium-low heat. Gently lift each disk with a spatula and place it in the frying pan. (Handle the dough with care so you don’t deflate it.) Cook them for about 2 minutes on each side until lightly browned on both sides. Work in batches.
  11. Place the muffins back on the baking sheet and bake them for 10-15 minutes until cooked through. (15 minutes in our electric oven.) Check for doneness with a probe thermometer. It should read 200 F.
  12. Transfer the muffins to a cooling rack and let them cool completely.
  13. Split the muffins with a fork and toast them in a toaster until the edges are lightly browned. Serve warm with your favorite topping.
