Thai Iced Tea

Alton Brown + Juli

Ingredients

  • 5 cup(s) water
  • 6 bag(s) black tea earl gray
  • 1 ounce(s) tamarind pulp smashed
  • 8 unit(s) cardamom
  • 8 unit(s) cloves
  • 2 unit(s) star anise
  • 1 unit(s) cinnamon stick
  • 1 teaspoon(s) vanilla extract
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.12 cup(s) sugar 1/8 cup
  • 0.5 to taste half and half

Directions

  1. Bring water to boil in a 2-quart saucepan. Remove from the boil and add tea bags, tamarind, cardamom, cloves, star anise, cinnamon, vanilla and salt.
  2. Cover pan with a lid and steep for 20 minutes.
  3. Remove tea bags and strain out spices. Add sugar and stir to dissolve.
  4. Chill for at least 8 hours. Serve over ice with a splash of half-and-half.
