Dried Pasta

The Glorious Pasta of Italy

Ingredients

    Directions

    1. use 4 quarts of water for each 1 pound of pasta.
    2. Use 1-2 T kosher salt for 4 qt water.
    3. Be sure the water is at a rolling boil when you add the pasta, and allow the water to return to a boil before you start timing the cooking.&nbsp; Whole-wheat pasta can go from underdone to overcooked quickly, so be vigilant.
    4. Do not add oil to the water, as it may prevent the sauce from clinging to the pasta.
    5. When you drain the pasta in a colander, always reserve some of the cooking water. A splash of cooking water can help bring the noodles and sauce together and prevent them from clumping.
    6. When dressing pasta with sauce, transfer the drained noodles to the pan holding the sauce and toss them together in the pan.
    7. Don't overdress your noodles or it will be overwhelmed by too much sauce.&nbsp; Dress the noodles lightly and spoon a little additional sauce on top.
    8. Warm your serving bowl or individual pasta bowls. Pasta turns cold quickly and a warmed bowl will keep the noodles warm. Pop the bowls into a low-temperature oven for 5 minutes or so to warm them up. It's one small step that can make a big difference.
