California Sunrise Salad with Sour Cream-Cilantro Dressing

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) vegetable oil
  • 4 unit(s) corn tortilla(s) cut into 1/8-inch by 2-inch strips
  • 1 unit(s) romaine leaves separated
  • 1 unit(s) radicchio shaved very thinly crosswise
  • 1 pint(s) cherry tomato(es)
  • 0.33 cup(s) olive(s)
  • 1 cup(s) monterey jack cheese coarsely grated
  • 1 unit(s) sour cream-cilantro dressing

Directions

  1. Heat 1 inch of oil in a deep-sided skillet over medium-high heat. In batches, fry the tortilla strips until very crisp. Drain on paper towels. Season with salt while still hot.
  2. Place the romaine leaves in a bowl and toss with the dressing. Arrange the romaine neatly in the center of a large plate to resemble a sun. Sprinkle the shaved radicchio over the top. Place the tomatoes and olives in the center of the romaine and radicchio arrangement. Sprinkle the salad with the cheese and surround the salad with a wreath of tortilla strips.
