Mayonnaise

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) egg yolks room temperature
  • 1 teaspoon(s) Dijon mustard room temperature
  • 0.33 cup(s) olive oil
  • 0.33 cup(s) vegetable oil
  • 0.5 unit(s) lemon(s) juiced, room temperature

Directions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together the yolk, mustard and about 1 T of the olive oil until an emulsion forms.
  2. Combine the remaining olive oil and the vegetable oil in a cup with a spout. Drop by drop, begin adding the oil to the emulsion while whisking constantly. Continue to add the oil in this manner, whisking constantly and increasing the flow to a thin, steady stream as it is incorporated, until the mixture has the consistency of mayonnaise.
  3. Season to taste with salt and pepper and the lemon juice.
