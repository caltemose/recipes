Warm Apple Streusel Tartlets

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) tart dough
  • 2 pound(s) granny smith apple(s)
  • 3.5 tablespoon(s) cinnamon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon(s) all-purpose flour
  • 4 ounce(s) dried fruit raisins, currants, cranberries, or cherries
  • 1.25 ounce(s) walnuts chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) butter softened
  • 0.67 cup(s) sugar
  • 1.5 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 1 to taste ground cinnamon

Directions

  1. Preheat an oven to 375 F. On a lightly floured work surface, roll out the dough until it is 1/8-inch thick. With a 5-inch cutter, cut the dough into eight circles and transfer the pastry circles to eight 4-1/2-inch tartlet pans. Push the dough into the crimps on the edges of the pans.
  2. Peel and core the apples and slice into quarters. Cut each quarter crosswise into thin slices. Place the apple slices in a bowl and toss with cinnamon sugar and flour. Stir in the raisins or currants and walnuts.
  3. In a food processor or by hand, mix the butter and sugar until light and creamy. Add the flour and mix until a rough, crumbly mixture forms (pulse in short bursts if using a food processor). Mix in the cinnamon. Set aside.
  4. Divide the apple mixture among the lined tartlet pans, packing the filling slightly. Sprinkle the tartlets with the streusel mixture, dividing evenly.
  5. Bake the tartlets for 1 to 1-1/4 hours until the apples are cooked and the streusel is golden brown. Let the tartlets stand at room temperature for 30 minutes before serving. Serve warm.
