Brussels Sprouts - Heavenly Orbs of Belgian Goodness

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound(s) brussels sprouts
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) sesame oil
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) fish sauce
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes
  • 2 ounce(s) peanuts roasted and chopped

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 425 F.
  2. Trim the dry ends from the sprouts and remove any yellow outer leaves ,then halve the sprouts longitudinally.
  3. Coat the sprouts with the olive oil and salt to taste in a large bowl and spread in an even layer on a baking sheet, placing the cut sides down. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes or until the sprouts are lightly browned and tender.
  4. Toss the warm sprouts with the sesame oil, fish sauce, red pepper flakes, and peanuts. Serve warm.
