Fedelini with Broccoli Rabe, Pancetta and Toasted Bread Crumbs

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.5 loaf bread country white
  • 1 unit(s) olive oil or spray
  • 1 tablespoon(s) cornstarch
  • 2.5 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 0.33 cup(s) evaporated skim milk
  • 2 ounce(s) pancetta cut into julienne
  • 0.75 cup(s) onion(s) diced
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) garlic minced
  • 1 pound(s) broccoli rabe chopped
  • 2 teaspoon(s) thyme chopped
  • 2 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes
  • 1 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 0.75 pound(s) fedelini dried, or thin spaghetti
  • 0.33 cup(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 3 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Remove the crust from the bread and discard. Cut the bread into large dice and process it in a food processor to medium-sized crumbs. Spread the crumbs on a baking sheet and spray them with the olive oil spray. Bake the crumbs until golden brown, turning frequently. Set aside to cool.
  2. In a small bowl or cup, mix the cornstarch with 2 tablespoons of the broth. Bring the remaining broth to a simmer in a small sauce pan. Add the cornstarch mixture and the evaporated milk to the simmering broth, stirring constantly until slightly thickened. Keep the sauce warm.
  3. Preheat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the pancetta and sauce until crisp and browned, about 5 minutes. Add the onion and garlic and saute until the onion is translucent. Add the broccoli rabe, thyme, and red pepper flakes and saute until the broccoli rabe is tender, about 15 minutes. Stir in the broth mixture and simmer until it is reduced to a sauce consistency. Taste and season with the lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water until slightly firm to the bite, al dente. Drain the pasta well and add it to the broccoli rabe mixture, tossing the ingredients together well.
  5. Divide the pasta mixture among 4 warm serving plates and sprinkle each serving with toasted bread crumbs, parmesan, and parsley.
