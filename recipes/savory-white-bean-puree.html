Savory White Bean Puree

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup(s) cannellini beans or other white beans, dreid
  • 6 cup(s) water
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 clove(s) garlic slivered
  • 1 sprig(s) thyme or rosemary
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil

Directions

  1. Rinse and pick over the beans. Soak the beans overnight in water to cover by several inches. Drain.
  2. In a large saucepan, combine the beans and water. Bring to a simmer and cook until tender, about 45 minutes to 1 hour. Add the salt, garlic, and herb sprig, and continue to simmer for 15 minutes. Remove the herb sprig and drain the beans, reserving the liquid.
  3. In a blender or food processor, puree the beans, garlic, and oil until smooth. Adjust the consistency with the reserved liquid. Serve with crostini.
