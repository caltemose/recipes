Walnut Liqueur

Vincent Schiavelli

Ingredients

  • 1 pound(s) walnuts raw, in halves and pieces
  • 2 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s)
  • 4.25 cup(s) 190-proof alcohol everclear
  • 1 bottle(s) white wine Sicilian or Italian
  • 2 cup(s) sugar

Directions

  1. Place the alcohol, walnuts and cinnamon sticks in a jar and macerate for 15 days.
  2. Move the liquid to a larger jar and add the white wine and sugar. Secure the lid. Turn the jar from time to time over the next 24 hours to dissolve the sugar.
  3. Sterilize the bottles and fill. This liqueur is about 92 proof.
  4. Do not jostle the bottle for about a week, until the frothy residue stops rising to the top. Skim off the residue.
