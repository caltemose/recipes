Black Bean Cakes with Fresh Salsa

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup(s) black beans dried
  • 4 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 4 tablespoon(s) yogurt plain, nonfat
  • 3 tablespoon(s) sour cream
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil plus 1/2 C for frying
  • 0.33 cup(s) onion(s) finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) garlic minced
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) chili powder
  • 2 unit(s) egg white(s) beaten until frothy
  • 3 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 0.25 cup(s) cilantro chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 unit(s) corn meal for dusting
  • 4 cup(s) fresh salsa

Directions

  1. Rinse and pick over the beans. Soak the beans overnight in water to cover by several inches. Drain.
  2. In a large saucepan, combine the beans and broth. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 50 to 60 minutes or until tender. The broth should reduce until it is almost completely absorbed at the end of the cooking time.
  3. In a small bowl, mix the yogurt and sour cream until blended. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
  4. In a medium saute pan or skillet over medium heat, heat 2 T of the olive oil and saute the onions and garlic until golden brown, about 8 minutes. Add the cumin and chili powder and saute for 1 minute. Add the onion mixture, black beans, egg white, lime juice, cilantro, and salt and mix well.
  5. Remove the beans from the heat and let cool to the touch. Form into 2-inch cakes and dust lightly with cornmeal. In a large saucepan or skillet over medium heat, heat the remaining olive oil and saute the cakes until a crust forms on both sides, about 5 minutes total cooking time. Serve immediately with the yogurt mixture and salsa.
