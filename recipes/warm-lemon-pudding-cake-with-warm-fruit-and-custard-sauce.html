Warm Lemon Pudding Cake with Warm Fruit and Custard Sauce

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter at room temperature
  • 1.25 cup(s) sugar
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 6 unit(s) egg yolks
  • 0.5 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 4 teaspoon(s) lemon zest
  • 0.5 cup(s) lemon juice
  • 2 cup(s) milk
  • 8 unit(s) egg white(s)
  • 4 cup(s) custard sauce
  • 1 unit(s) poached dried fruit

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Liberally butter 12 small ramekins or custard cups and dust with granulated sugar. Place 3 layers of paper towels in the bottom of a shallow baking dish. Set the ramekins in the pan.
  2. In a large bowl, cream the butter, sugar, and salt together until smooth. Beat in the egg yolks. Scrape down the sides of the bowl to blend the batter properly. Add the flour and mix until smooth. Stir in the lemon zest and juice. Add the milk and mix thoroughly.
  3. In a large bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff glossy peaks form. Fold into the batter.
  4. Ladle the batter into the ramekins. Carefully pour boiling water around the ramekins to reach halfway up the sides. Bake the pudding cakes until they are set and the center springs back when lightly pressed, about 30 minutes. Let sit in the water bath for 10 minutes to finish cooking.
  5. Serve the warm pudding with custard sauce and poached fruit.
  6. Instead of preparing custard sauce, you can melt a good-quality vanilla ice cream in a double boiler. Cover and refrigerate the melted ice cream for at least 1 hour. Stir well before serving.
