Coconut Rice

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 2 cup(s) white rice
  • 3 cup(s) water
  • 2 cup(s) coconut milk unsweetened
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) salt or to taste

Directions

  1. In a saucepan, heat the butter over medium heat. Add the rice and stir until the grains are coated with the butter. Add the water, coconut milk, and salt, and stir with a fork. Bring the liquid just to a boil and reduce the heat so that the mixture simmers gently. Simmer the rice for approximately 16 minutes or until the grains are tender. Fluff the rice with a fork to separate the grains.
