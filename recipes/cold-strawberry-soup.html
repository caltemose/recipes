Cold Strawberry Soup

CIA

Ingredients

  • 4 cup(s) strawberries
  • 0.33 cup(s) sugar
  • 0.25 cup(s) strawberry liqueur
  • 3 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 3 cup(s) apple juice
  • 1 teaspoon(s) lemon juice
  • 0.5 cup(s) honey

Directions

  1. Reserve 1 large berry for garnish. In a large nonreactive bowl, combine the remaining strawberries, sugar, and liqueur and refrigerate for 2 to 24 hours. Puree the berries.
  2. Add all the remaining ingredients to the berries. Mix well, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 or 3 hours.
  3. Serve the soup very cold in chilled cups. Cut the reserved berry into paper-thin slices. Garnish each serving with a slice of strawberry.
