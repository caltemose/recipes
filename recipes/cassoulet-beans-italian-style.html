Cassoulet Beans Italian-Style

CIA

Ingredients

  • 8 ounce(s) cannellini beans soaked overnight in water to cover by 3 inches
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 unit(s) onion(s) medium, minced
  • 8 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 1 stalk(s) celery large, finely chopped
  • 5 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 3 sprig(s) thyme
  • 1 sprig(s) rosemary
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 2 teaspoon(s) black pepper

Directions

  1. Drain the beans, rinse them, drain them again and set them aside.
  2. In a large saucepan, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the onions and sauce until translucent. Add the garlic and celery and sauce until the celery is tender. Add the beans and enough broth to cover the beans by 1 inch. Bring the liquid to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to low and simmer the beans for 20 minutes. While the beans are simmering, skim any scum and oil that floats to the surface. Add additional stock to the beans as necessary to keep them from boiling dry.
  3. Add the bay leaf, thyme, and rosemary. Continue to simmer the beans until they are just tender, approximately 1 hour, adding salt and pepper after 30 minutes.
