Apple Spice Bundt Cake with Rum Glaze

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) apple(s) medium granny smith
  • 434 gram(s) all-purpose flour
  • 6 gram(s) baking soda
  • 8 gram(s) ground grains of paradise
  • 1 gram(s) nutmeg ground
  • 1 gram(s) ground ginger
  • 1 gram(s) cardamom ground
  • 1 gram(s) allspice ground
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) rosemary ground
  • 0.12 teaspoon(s) star anise ground
  • 3 gram(s) salt
  • 336 gram(s) butter at room temperature
  • 224 gram(s) sugar
  • 196 gram(s) brown sugar light brown
  • 3 unit(s) egg(s) large, at room temperature
  • 1 teaspoon(s) vanilla extract
  • 100 gram(s) pecans chopped
  • 100 gram(s) candied ginger finely chopped
  • 336 gram(s) powdered sugar
  • 14 gram(s) rum
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 28 gram(s) demerara sugar

Directions

  1. Set a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 325 F. Lightly butter a bundt pan and dust with flour, tapping out the excess.
  2. Chop one of the apples into 1/4-inch dice, leaving the peel on. Grate the other apple on the large holes of a box grater. Set both aside.
  3. Combine the flour, baking soda, grains of paradise, nutmeg, ground ginger, cardamom, allspice, rosemary, star anise, and salt in a large bowl.
  4. Install the paddle attachment on a stand mixer and cream together the butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar at medium-high speed until fluffy and pale.
  5. Reduce the speed to low and add the eggs, one at a time, mixing thoroughly after each addition, followed by the vanilla.
  6. Add the flour mixture in three batches.
  7. Add the apples, pecans, and crystallized ginger.
  8. Transfer the batter to the prepared bundt pan and bake for 75 minutes, rotating the pan after 30 minutes. Cover the cake during the last 15 minutes of baking if the top gets too brown. The cake is done when it pulls away from the sides of the pan, springs back when pressed, or the internal temperature reaches 205 F.
  9. Cool the cake in the pan on a rack for 30 minutes. Invert and remove the cake from the pan. Cool completely on the rack before glazing.
  10. Combine the powdered sugar, rum, salt, and 28 grams water in a small mixing bowl. Whisk with a fork until smooth.
  11. Drizzle the glaze onto the bundt cake. Sprinkle with the demerara sugar.
  12. Allow the glaze to set for 30 minutes before serving.
