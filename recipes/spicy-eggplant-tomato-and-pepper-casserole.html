Spicy Eggplant, Tomato and Pepper Casserole

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) olive oil
  • 3 unit(s) eggplant(s) medium Asian, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch-thick slices
  • 1 unit(s) zucchini cut into 1/2-inch-thick slices
  • 2 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) seeded and finely diced
  • 2 unit(s) tomato(es) cut into 1/2-inch-thick slices
  • 1 tablespoon(s) green onion chopped
  • 0.33 cup(s) sesame oil plus more for sprinkling
  • 1 teaspoon(s) garlic minced
  • 1 teaspoon(s) ginger minced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) hot bean paste
  • 0.75 cup(s) rice wine vinegar
  • 0.5 cup(s) sugar
  • 0.5 cup(s) soy sauce
  • 0.5 cup(s) sherry
  • 0.5 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 1 tablespoon(s) cornstarch dissolved in water

Directions

  1. In a large skillet, heat a small amount of oil over medium-high heat. In batches, add the eggplant slices and saute for 1 minute on each side, adding more oil as necessary. Drain the eggplant on paper towels. Add the zucchini to the skillet and saute for 1 minute on each side, adding more oil if necessary, and drain on paper towels. Add the bell peppers to the skillet and saute for 2 minutes, adding more oil if necessary, and drain on paper towels.
  2. In a greased casserole, layer half of the eggplant. Top with a layer each of the tomatoes, zucchini and peppers. Sprinkle with the green onions. Finish with a layer of the remaining eggplant.
  3. Preheat the oven to 400 F. In a skillet, heat the sesame oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and ginger and saute for 1 minute. Add the hot bean paste, vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, sherry, and broth and simmer for 1 minute. Add the dissolved cornstarch and stir until thickened. Pour the sauce over the eggplant in the casserole. Pierce the eggplant with a fork and sprinkle with a small amount of the sesame oil.
  4. Place the casserole in the oven and bake for 30 minutes or until the vegetables are tender. Serve portions on warm plates accompanied by hot white rice.
