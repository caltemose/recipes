Prawn, Chicken, Coconut, and Lemongrass Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 3.5 cup(s) coconut milk
  • 1.25 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 12 ounce(s) chicken cut into slivers
  • 3 stalk(s) lemongrass heavily bruised
  • 0.5 inch ginger cut into thin matchsticks
  • 8 ounce(s) prawns raw, shelled
  • 1 unit(s) red chiles seeded and diced
  • 5 unit(s) scallions sliced
  • 1 to taste lime juice
  • 3 tablespoon(s) cilantro coarsely chopped
  • 3 tablespoon(s) fish sauce

Directions

  1. Put the coconut milk and stock into a pan. Bring to a boil. Add the chicken, lemongrass, and ginger and lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Cook for 10 minutes, uncovered.
  2. Add the prawns and cook for another 2-3 minutes, then add all the remaining ingredients. Remove the lemongrass and discard. Taste and add more lime juice or fish sauce, if needed, then serve.
