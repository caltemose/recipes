Spicy-Sour Drink

The Indian Vegan Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup(s) mint leaves, loosely packed
  • 1 tablespoon(s) tamarind paste or 1/2 C tamarind sauce
  • 1 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 2 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 teaspoon(s) cayenne pepper
  • 6 cup(s) water
  • 1 tablespoon(s) sugar
  • 0.25 cup(s) lime juice
  • 1 unit(s) carrot(s) carrot or celery sticks for garnish

Directions

  1. Blend mint leaves, tamarind sauce, cumin powder, black salt, salt, and cayenne pepper in Cuisinart until a paste-like consistency forms.
  2. Combine mint paste and water. Mix well.
  3. Refrigerate for 4 hours or more. Stir before serving. Serve in small glasses with a carrot stick for stirring.
