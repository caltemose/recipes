Ginger Tea

Ingredients

  • 2 inch ginger scrubbed and thinly sliced
  • 1 cup(s) water

Directions

  1. In a sauce pan, combine ginger and water. Bring to a boil.
  2. Serve with a thin round of lemon or orange or a light drizzle of honey.
