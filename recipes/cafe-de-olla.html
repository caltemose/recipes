Cafe de Olla

Ingredients

  • 2 quart(s) water
  • 0.5 cup(s) brown sugar or 3 oz piloncillo
  • 2 unit(s) cinnamon stick(s)
  • 1 cup(s) ground coffee dark roast

Directions

  1. In a saucepan over medium-high heat, heat the water, sugar and cinnamon and stir until sugar has dissolved. Increase heat and bring to a boil, then add ground coffee, stir and lower heat to a simmer. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
  2. Remove from heat, let sit for 2 minutes. Then strain coffee into warmed cups.
