Sangria

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) fresh fruit chopped, plus more for garnish
  • 3 tablespoon(s) sugar
  • 1 cup(s) brandy
  • 2 bottle(s) red wine
  • 0.5 cup(s) orange juice
  • 0.5 cup(s) lemon-lime soda

Directions

  1. Combine fruit, sugar and brandy is a 3-quart pitcher and stir until sugar dissolves. Refrigerate 6-8 hours.
  2. When you’re ready to serve, add wine, orange juice and soda to pitcher and stir to combine. Pour over ice and garnish with fruit.
