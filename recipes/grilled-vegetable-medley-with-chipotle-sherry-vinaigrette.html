Grilled Vegetable Medley with Chipotle-Sherry Vinaigrette

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 pound(s) eggplant(s) about 1
  • 1 pound(s) zucchini about 2
  • 1 pound(s) yellow squash about 2
  • 1 pound(s) portobello mushroom(s)
  • 1.5 pound(s) red bell pepper(s) about 3
  • 5 unit(s) roma tomato(es)
  • 10 unit(s) green onion
  • 0.5 cup(s) olive oil
  • 0.5 bunch thyme
  • 1 unit(s) chipotle-sherry vinaigrette #1
  • 1.5 pound(s) yellow bell peppers about 3

Directions

  1. Cut the eggplant into 1/2-inch-thick rounds, sprinkle it generously with salt, and let it stand in a colander for 30 minutes. Rinse the eggplant to remove the salt and blot it dry with paper towels, pressing to remove the excess moisture.
  2. Preheat the grill to medium-high. Cut the zucchini and yellow squash on the bias into long slices. Remove the stems from the portobellos. Cut the bell peppers into eighths. Core and have the tomatoes. Trim the root ends of the green onions.
  3. In a bowl, combine the olive oil, thyme, salt, and pepper. Brush the vegetables with this mixture. Grill the eggplant, yellow squash, zucchini, and mushrooms for about 4 to 5 minutes per side. Grill the peppers for about 5 to 7 minutes per side. Grill the tomato and green onions for about 3 to 5 minutes. All vegetables should be tender, have nice grill marks, and be very hot.
  4. Slice the portobellos to make 10 portions and divide among 10 serving plates. Arrange 2 to 3 slices of eggplant, zucchini, and yellow squash on the plates. Add 2 strips each of red pepper and yellow pepper, a grilled tomato half, and a green onion. Drizzle the vegetables with the vinaigrette and serve warm or at room temperature.
