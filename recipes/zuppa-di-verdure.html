Zuppa di Verdure

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 7 ounce(s) cannellini beans soaked overnight in water, or a 14-oz can
  • 1 unit(s) bouquet garni rosemary, thyme, 2 bay leaves
  • 4 tablespoon(s) olive oil plus extra to serve
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) chopped
  • 3 unit(s) carrot(s) diced
  • 2 stalk(s) celery diced
  • 2 unit(s) leek(s) very thinly sliced
  • 3 clove(s) garlic chopped
  • 1 unit(s) red chiles dried, finely chopped
  • 3 unit(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) tomato puree
  • 4 unit(s) cabbage cavalo nero or curly kale leaves, tough stalks removed and leaves coarsely chopped
  • 6 slice(s) bread stale
  • 1 clove(s) garlic cut in half

Directions

  1. Drain the beans and place them in a large pan with the bouquet garni and twice their volume of cold water. Bring to a boil, then boil hard for 10 minutes. Lower the heat, and simmer gently for 1-2 hours or until tender, adding more water if needed. Remove the pan from the heat, discard the bouquet garni, and leave the beans to cool in their cooking water.
  2. Heat the oil in a large heavy pot, then add the onion, carrots, celery, leeks, garlic, and chile. Cover with a lid and sweat over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring once or twice. Add the tomatoes and tomato puree and cook over a moderate heat for 3-4 minutes. Add the beans, their cooking water, and enough water to cover the vegetables generously. Season well with salt and pepper. Bring to a boil and simmer gently for 20 minutes or until the vegetables are tender. Puree half the soup in a blender, then return to the pan. Add the cavolo nero and simmer for 10 minutes or until tender. Adjust the seasonings.
  3. Rub the bread with the garlic and arrange in a warm shallow casserole dish. Spoon the soup and serve with a bottle of high-quality olive oil out on the table for people to help themselves.
