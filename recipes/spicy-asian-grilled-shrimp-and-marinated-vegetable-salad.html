Spicy Asian Grilled Shrimp and Marinated Vegetable Salad

CIA

Ingredients

  • 24 unit(s) shrimp medium, peeled
  • 3 tablespoon(s) rice wine vinegar plus 2 t
  • 3 teaspoon(s) garlic minced
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) five-spice powder
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) fish sauce
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) Tabasco sauce
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) sesame oil toasted
  • 0.33 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 2 teaspoon(s) soy sauce
  • 2 teaspoon(s) shallot(s) minced
  • 2 teaspoon(s) grainy mustard
  • 1 tablespoon(s) chive(s) chopped
  • 1.5 cup(s) carrot(s) julienned
  • 1.5 cup(s) daikon radish julienned
  • 2 tablespoon(s) pickled ginger minced
  • 1 to taste nori (seaweed) toasted, julienned, for garnish
  • 1 to taste sesame seeds toasted

Directions

  1. With a sharp paring knife, make a horizontal cut lengthwise down the back of each shrimp to butterfly it, cutting almost but not entirely through the shrimp. Remove and discard the dark vein that runs down the length of the shrimp.
  2. Place the shrimp in a glass bowl. Add 2 teaspoons of the vinegar, 1-1/2 teaspoons of the garlic, the five-spice powder, ginger, tabasco, fish sauce, and oil to the bowl. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  3. In a medium bowl, combine the broth, the remaining 3 tablespoons vinegar, soy sauce, shallots, mustard, and the remaining 1-1/2 tablespoons garlic. While whisking, slowly drizzle in the oil until blended. Stir in the chives. Add the carrot, daikon, and pickled ginger to the bowl and toss well. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Preheat a gas or charcoal grill to medium. Drain the shrimp and place them on the grill. Grill the shrimp until thoroughly cooked, about 2 minutes per side. Take care not to overcook.
  5. Drain the marinated vegetables and divide them among 4 or 5 serving plates. Arrange the grilled shrimp on top of the vegetables and serve immediately, garnished with the nori and sesame seeds.
