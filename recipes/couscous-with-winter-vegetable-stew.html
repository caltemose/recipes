Couscous with Winter Vegetable Stew

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) saffron
  • 1 stick(s) cinnamon
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) turmeric
  • 1.5 unit(s) yellow onion(s) quartered
  • 1 clove(s) garlic chopped
  • 1 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) seeded
  • 0.5 bunch cilantro tied together
  • 2 unit(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and quartered
  • 3 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 2 unit(s) carrot(s) cut into 1-inch lengths
  • 1 unit(s) turnip(s) small, peeled and quartered
  • 0.5 pound(s) butternut squash peeled, halved, and seeded
  • 2 unit(s) zucchini small, trimmed
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt plus salt to taste
  • 1.75 cup(s) water
  • 1.75 cup(s) couscous
  • 1 to taste cilantro
  • 1 to taste parsley
  • 0.5 cup(s) broth from the strew
  • 1 teaspoon(s) harissa
  • 1 teaspoon(s) lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped

Directions

  1. In a large soup pot over medium heat, melt the butter. Add the saffron, cumin, cinnamon sticks, turmeric, onions, garlic, jalapeño, cilantro, and tomatoes. Stir until well mixed. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes. Add the vegetable stock, cover, and simmer for another 3 minutes to meld the flavors. Add the carrots and turnips, cover, and continue to simmer until tender when pierced with a fork, about 30 minutes longer.
  2. Cut the butternut squash and zucchini into 1-inch chunks and add them to the stew. Cover and simmer until tender when pressed with a fork, about 20 minutes. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Discard the cinnamon sticks, chili pepper, and cilantro bundle.
  3. About 10 minutes before the stew is ready, in a saucepan, bring the water to a boil. Remove from the heat and stir in the couscous and the 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes.
  4. To make the harissa sauce, in a bowl, stir together the broth, harissa, lemon juice, cilantro, and salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Uncover the couscous and fluff with a fork. Spread the couscous on a large platter and make a well in the center. Using a slotted spoon, place the vegetables in the well. Moisten the couscous with a few spoonfuls of the broth. Serve with the harissa sauce and the remaining broth in separate bowls on the side.
