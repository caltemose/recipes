Chinese Egg Drop Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 quart(s) chicken broth
  • 1 clove(s) garlic crushed
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ginger grated
  • 2 unit(s) scallions chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) soy sauce
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) five-spice powder
  • 2 unit(s) corn on the cob, or 7 oz can
  • 2 handful(s) spinach baby leaves
  • 2 tablespoon(s) cornstarch
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s) beaten

Directions

  1. Put the stock in a pan with the garlic, ginger, scallions, soy sauce, and five-spice powder. If using fresh corn, place each corn on the cob on its end and slide a sharp knife down the length to remove all the kernels. Add the kernels to the pan, bring to a boil, reduce the heat, cover, and simmer gently for 20 minutes.
  2. Add the canned sweet corn, if using, including any liquid, and the spinach. Bring back to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer for 1 minute or until the spinach has just wilted. Taste and add more soy sauce if necessary.
  3. Blend the cornstarch with 4 tablespoons water and stir in. Bring back to a boil and simmer, stirring, for 1 minute to thicken slightly. Gradually trickle in the beaten egg, stirring gently, so that it forms thin strands. Serve in warm soup bowls.
