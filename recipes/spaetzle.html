Spaetzle

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 6 tablespoon(s) milk
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) nutmeg
  • 1.25 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter 2-4 T

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the eggs, milk, salt, pepper, and nutmeg and mix well with a wooden spoon. Add the flour and stir until smooth. Let the dough stand for 10 minutes.
  2. With a spaetzle maker (or other shaping technique), drop the dough into a large pot of boiling salted water. Simmer the spaetzle until they rise to the surface of the water. With a strainer, remove the spaetzle from the water and place them in a bowl of cold water to stop the cooking. Drain the spaetzle well.
  3. When ready to serve, saute the spaetzle in butter until heated through and lightly browned.
