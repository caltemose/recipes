Coffee Liqueur

Vincent Schiavelli

Ingredients

  • 3 cup(s) ground coffee espresso beans
  • 2 cup(s) 190-proof alcohol everclear, plus 2 T
  • 6 cup(s) water
  • 1.5 cup(s) sugar
  • 1 to taste heavy cream to float on top

Directions

  1. Place all the ingredients except the cream in an appropriate-sized canning jar. Secure the lid and macerate for 6 days.
  2. The day the maceration is completed, sterilize the bottles. Place them in a sink and fill with boiling water. After a few minutes, drain them and let them cool.
  3. Filter the alcohol through a clean “gold” type filter into a clean jar. When filtering be sure to use a filter normally used for brewing coffee, so as not to taint the one used for other liqueur flavors.
  4. Boil water. Remove from stove and add sugar to dissolve, stirring slowly.
  5. Bottle the liqueur when ingredients have cooled. Let the air bubbles escape before corking.
  6. Serve in cordial glasses, with a small layer of heavy cream floated on top. This liqueur is approx 42 proof.
