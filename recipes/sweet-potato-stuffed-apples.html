Sweet Potato-Stuffed Apples

CIA

Ingredients

  • 4 unit(s) sweet potato(es) medium, scrubbed
  • 2 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 0.25 cup(s) brown sugar or to taste
  • 3 tablespoon(s) butter melted
  • 8 unit(s) apple(s) baking apples, cored

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 F.
  2. Lightly rub the sweet potatoes with oil and bake them in the oven until very tender, about 45 to 50 minutes. Remove the potatoes from the oven and reduce the heat to 350 F.
  3. Remove the skin from the sweet potatoes and puree the flesh in a food processor. Place the pureed in a bowl with the brown sugar, melted butter, and salt and pepper. Transfer the mixture to a pastry bag.
  4. Pipe the sweet potato mixture into the cored apples, dividing evenly. Place the apples in a lightly buttered baking dish. Bake until the apples are tender and the filling is very hot, about 15 to 20 minutes. Serve warm.
