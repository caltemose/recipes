Corn Salsa

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 2 cup(s) corn about 3 ears
  • 2 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) seeded and minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lime juice
  • 0.5 cup(s) red onion(s) finely chopped
  • 0.33 cup(s) cilantro

Directions

  1. Fill a saucepan three-fourths full with water and bring to a boil. Add the corn kernels and boil for 30 seconds. Drain, place in a bowl and let cool. Add the chili peppers, lime juice, red onion, cilantro, and salt and pepper to taste. Mix well and let sit for 1 hour.
