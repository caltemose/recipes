Algerian Chickpea Bread

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.33 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 3 tablespoon(s) water
  • 1 teaspoon(s) yeast
  • 1 tablespoon(s) honey
  • 1.75 cup(s) water
  • 2 cup(s) unbleached flour
  • 0.5 cup(s) multi-grain flour
  • 0.5 cup(s) dark rye flour
  • 0.75 cup(s) semolina flour
  • 0.33 cup(s) whole wheat flour
  • 2 tablespoon(s) salt
  • 1 cup(s) chickpeas cooked, drained
  • 2 packet(s) yeast
  • 0.5 cup(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. To make the sponge: In a small bowl, combine the flour with the water and yeast. Stir the mixture until blended, cover and let sit in a warm place for 24 hours.
  2. In the bowl of a heavy-duty electric mixer, stir the sponge with the honey, water, and yeast. Let sit for 10 minutes or until bubbly. Add the flours and mix the dough on slow speed for 4 minutes. Add the salt and mix on high speed for 10 minutes. Add the remaining ingredients and mix on high speed for 9 minutes.
  3. To make by hand, stir the sponge, honey, water and yeast together. Let sit for 10 minutes or until bubbly. Whisk in the flours 1/2 cup at a time until the mixture is thick, then sift in the salt and the remaining flours 1/2 cup at a time. Turn out on a lightly floured board and knead until smooth and elastic, about 8 minutes.
  4. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, turn to coat, and cover with a damp cloth. Let rise in a warm place for 1-1/2 hours, or until doubled. Punch the dough down and turn out on a lightly floured board. Cut the dough in half and form it into smooth balls. Cover the balls with a damp cloth and let sit on the work surface for 20 minutes.
  5. Preheat the oven to 500 F. Using a large plate, flatten the dough balls into disks. Dust a baking sheet with a little multigrain flour and set the disks on the baking sheet. Cover them again and let the dough rise for 30 minutes.
  6. Place the bread in the oven, mist it with water, and bake for 30 minutes or until golden and baked through.
