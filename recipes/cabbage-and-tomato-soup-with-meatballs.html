Cabbage and Tomato Soup with Meatballs

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 clove(s) garlic crushed
  • 0.5 unit(s) red onion(s) finely chopped
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) paprika hot, smoked
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon(s) thyme dried
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 14 ounce(s) ground beef
  • 1 unit(s) egg(s) large
  • 3 tablespoon(s) all-purpose flour
  • 3 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 2 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 0.5 unit(s) red onion(s) coarsely chopped
  • 1 clove(s) garlic crushed
  • 9 ounce(s) tomato(es) seeded and chopped
  • 1 head(s) cabbage halved, core and ribs discarded, and leaves sliced into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 quart(s) beef broth
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley finely chopped, to garnish
  • 3 tablespoon(s) parmeggiano grated, to garnish

Directions

  1. For the meatballs, put the first nine ingredients in a bowl, season, and mix well. Shape into walnut-sized balls and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a large deep pan over medium heat. Add in the onion, garlic, and tomatoes. Cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring. Add the cabbage and stir for 3 minutes. Stir in the stock and simmer for 15-20 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, sift the flour onto a plate and coat the meatballs. Heat the oil and butter in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the meatballs and cook for 3 minutes until they are a rich brown all over. Add to the soup and simmer for 15-20 minutes. Serve scattered with parsley and parmesan.
