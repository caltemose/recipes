Porco com Ameijoas a Alentejana

Savoring Spain and Portugal

Ingredients

  • 2 pound(s) pork shoulder or butt, boneless, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 cup(s) dry white wine
  • 3 tablespoon(s) paprika sweet
  • 1 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 2 unit(s) cloves whole cloves
  • 5 clove(s) garlic chopped
  • 4 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 unit(s) yellow onion(s) chopped
  • 4 unit(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and diced
  • 2 pound(s) clams small, well scrubbed
  • 3 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped
  • 21 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. Place the pork in a non aluminum container and add the 1 cup whine, paprika, bay leaf, cloves, and 3 of the garlic cloves. Toss well to mix evenly, cover, and refrigerate overnight.
  2. The next day, drain the pork, reserving the marinade. Pat the meat dry.
  3. In a large frying pan over medium heat, warm 2 T olive oil. Working in batches, brown the pork cubes on all sides until golden brown, about 5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer to a plate.
  4. In a large saucepan over medium heat, warm 2 T olive oli. Add the onions and sate until soft, 8-10 minutes. Add the remaining 2 cloves garlic and the tomatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes are soft, about 5 minutes. Add the browned pork to the onion mixture along with the reserved marinade, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook until the pork is tender, about 1 hour. Check from time to time and add more wine if the pork threatens to scorch.
  5. Add the clams, discarding any that are open or have broken shells, cover, raise the heat to high, and cook until the clams open, about 5 minutes. (Discard any clams that fail to open.)
  6. Transfer the pork and clams to a warmed serving dish and sprinkler with cilantro and parsley. Serve immediately.
