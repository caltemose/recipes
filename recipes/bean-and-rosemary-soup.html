Bean and Rosemary Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 unit(s) onion(s) finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) rosemary finely chopped
  • 1 handful(s) sage a few fresh leaves, finely chopped
  • 4 stalk(s) celery finely chopped
  • 3 clove(s) garlic finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) tomato puree
  • 1 can(s) cannellini beans or borlotti, 28 oz, drained and rinsed and drained again
  • 1.25 quart(s) chicken broth hot
  • 5.5 pound(s) potato cut into chunky pieces

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan, add the onions, and cook over low heat for 6-8 minutes or until soft and translucent. Season well with salt and pepper, then stir in the rosemary, sage, celery, and garlic and cook over very low heat, stirring occasionally, for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the tomato puree and beans and cook gently for 5 minutes. Pour in the stock, bring to a boil, then add the potatoes and simmer gently for 15 minutes or until cooked. Taste, season with salt and pepper, if needed, and serve with a drizzle of olive oil.
