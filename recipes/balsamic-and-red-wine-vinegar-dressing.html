Balsamic and Red Wine Vinegar Dressing

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 5 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon(s) balsamic vinegar

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, and balsamic vinegar. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
