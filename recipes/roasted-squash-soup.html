Roasted Squash Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound(s) winter squash pumpkin, butternut, acorn, seeds and rinds removed and cut into chunks
  • 1 pound(s) tomato(es) coarsely chopped
  • 6 clove(s) garlic unpeeled
  • 1 sprig(s) rosemary
  • 2 sprig(s) thyme
  • 1 unit(s) red onion(s) cut into 8 wedges
  • 4 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1.2 quart(s) vegetable stock hot
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 clove(s) garlic chopped
  • 1 unit(s) red chiles seeded and coarsely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) egg yolks
  • 1 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 0.5 cup(s) vegetable oil
  • 4 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 12 unit(s) croutes
  • 1 to taste gruyere cheese or compte, grated, to serve

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 F. Put the first seven ingredients into a roasting pan and turn to coat in oil. Roast, turning occasionally, for 45 minutes or until everything is very tender. Discard the herb stems and scrape the rest into a blender. Add the stock and process until smooth. You may need to do this in batches. Pour into a large pot and season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
  2. Meanwhile, make a rouille. Soak the saffron in 1 tablespoon of warm water. Pound the garlic, chile, and a pinch of salt to a paste in a mortar, then work in the egg yolk and vinegar. Mix the two oils, then drop them in, whisking constantly. When one-third of the oil has been incorporated, increase the flow to a slow trickle. Stir in the drained saffron, then adjust the seasonings.
  3. To serve, reheat the soup, then ladle into bowls. Pass the croutes, the rouille, and the grated cheese around separately, so everyone can smear the croutes liberally with the rouille, float them in the steaming soup, and then finish with a sprinkling of grated cheese.
