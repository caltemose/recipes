Lemon Tart - Healthy

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.75 cup(s) self-rising flour plus 2.5 T
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) baking powder
  • 0.25 cup(s) sugar plus 1.5 t
  • 1 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) lemon zest
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoon(s) water
  • 1 teaspoon(s) plain gelatin granulated
  • 1.5 cup(s) lemon juice
  • 1.5 cup(s) sugar
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) lemon zest
  • 4 unit(s) egg(s)
  • 2 teaspoon(s) vanilla extract

Directions

  1. In a food processor or by hand, mix together the flour and baking powder. Add the sugar, egg, lemon zest, and vanilla and mix until blended. Mix in the oil. Add the dry ingredients to the egg mixture and mix until just combined. Cover the dough and refrigerate for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 425 F. Press the dough on the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch fluted tart pan with a removable bottom. Prick the bottom with a fork, line the crust with foil, and weight it down with dried beans or pie weights. Bake the crust until golden brown, about 12 minutes. Cool completely.
  3. Place the water in a small dish and sprinkle with the gelatin; set aside for 5 minutes. Heat the gelatin mixture in a double boiler over simmering water or in a microwave on high power for 10 to 15 seconds.
  4. In a small sauce pan, combine the lemon juice, sugar, and lemon zest and bring to a simmer over medium heat. In a bowl, beat the eggs, then quickly stir about one-third of the lemon mixture into the eggs. Whisk the egg-lemon mixture into the remaining lemon mixture in the sauce pan and heat, whisking constantly, until the mixture starts to simmer very gently and begins to thicken.
  5. Strain the egg-lemon mixture through a fine-meshed sieve into a bowl and stir in the vanilla and melted gelatin. Cool the mixture to room temperature.
  6. Pour the lemon curd evenly into the crust and smooth the surface. Cover the tart and chill it for several hours or overnight before serving. Cut into wedges to serve.
