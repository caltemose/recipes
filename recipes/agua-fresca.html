Agua Fresca

Ingredients

  • 3 cup(s) fresh fruit cut into chunks
  • 2 unit(s) lime(s) juiced
  • 3 cup(s) water
  • 3 tablespoon(s) sugar
  • 1 pinch salt

Directions

  1. Place ingredients in blender and puree until very smooth. Chill. Serve over ice.
