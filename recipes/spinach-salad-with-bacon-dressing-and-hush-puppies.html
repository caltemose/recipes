Spinach Salad with Bacon Dressing and Hush Puppies

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound(s) spinach washed well
  • 6 ounce(s) mushrooms
  • 0.25 pound(s) radicchio thinly sliced
  • 1 unit(s) bacon dressing
  • 30 unit(s) hush puppies

Directions

  1. Place the spinach, mushrooms, and radicchio in a large bowl. Pour the warm dressing over the top and toss quickly until the spinach is slightly wilted.
  2. Divide the salad among 10 salad plates and garnish with the reserved bacon. Place 3 hush puppies on each plate. Serve immediately.
