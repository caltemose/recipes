Portobello Mushrooms with Tuscan Bean Salad and Celery Juice

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.33 cup(s) cannellini beans dried, soaked overnight in water to cover by 3 inches
  • 2 cup(s) vegetable stock or water, 2-3 cups
  • 0.25 cup(s) carrot(s) finely diced
  • 0.25 cup(s) celery finely diced
  • 0.5 cup(s) red bell pepper(s) finely diced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) green onion tops only, thinly sliced
  • 0.5 cup(s) champagne vinaigrette
  • 1 tablespoon(s) chive(s) chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 4 unit(s) portobello mushroom(s) caps, wiped clean
  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 0.33 cup(s) radicchio shredded
  • 2 teaspoon(s) cilantro chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) celery juice

Directions

  1. Drain the beans and rinse them with cold water. Combine the beans and 2 cups of the broth in a large saucepan and bring to a simmer. Simmer until the beans are tender, 40 minutes to 1 hour. Add more liquid as necessary to keep the beans submerged during cooking. Turn off the heat and let the beans cool in the cooking liquid.
  2. Drain the beans and transfer them to a bowl. Add the carrot, celery, red pepper, yellow pepper, green onion tops, champagne vinaigrette, chives, parsley, and salt and pepper and toss well to mix the ingredients. Let the mixture stand at room temperature for 2 hours to blend the flavors.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Place the mushroom caps on a baking sheet and brush them with the olive oil. Cover the pan with foil and roast the mushrooms until tender, about 15 to 20 minutes. Increase the oven temperature to broil. Remove the foil from the pan and broil the mushrooms for about 2 minutes on each side until browned.
  4. Divide the bean salad among 4 small plates, mounding the salad in the center of the plate. Cut each mushroom cap into 1/2-inch-thick slices and arrange the slices around the bean salad. Garnish each plate with radicchio and cilantro and drizzle with the celery juice. Serve immediately.
