Buttermilk Lassi

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 12 ounce(s) mango 2 large fruit, cubed
  • 2 cup(s) buttermilk
  • 1 tablespoon(s) ginger grated
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) chili pepper powdered
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender. Puree until smooth. Serve immediately.
