Pistachio Cream

Fran's Olive Oil Cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2 ounce(s) pistachios
  • 2 tablespoon(s) powdered sugar
  • 0.25 cup(s) milk

Directions

  1. Put the ingredients in a mixer and blend to a smooth cream. Squeeze the cream through a fine sieve.
