Champagne Vinaigrette

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) cornstarch
  • 0.5 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 0.25 cup(s) champagne vinegar
  • 0.25 cup(s) olive oil
  • 2 tablespoon(s) basil finely chopped
  • 1 pinch salt

Directions

  1. In a small bowl or cup, mix the cornstarch with 2 teaspoons of the broth and stir until smooth.
  2. In a saucepan, bring the remaining broth to a boil and stir in the cornstarch mixture. Cook, stirring constantly, until the broth has thickened. Remove the mixture from the heat, stir in the vinegar, and cool completely. While whisking, slowly drizzle in the oil until incorporated. Stir in the basil and salt and refrigerate until needed. Whisk the dressing just before serving.
