Bacon Dressing

CIA

Ingredients

  • 8 ounce(s) bacon cut into small dice
  • 3 tablespoon(s) shallot(s) minced
  • 1 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 0.5 cup(s) brown sugar plus 1 T
  • 6 tablespoon(s) apple cider vinegar
  • 6 tablespoon(s) vegetable stock thickened with cornstarch
  • 1 unit(s) cornstarch
  • 6 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) black pepper

Directions

  1. Prepare the vegetable stock: place the broth in a saucepan and bring to a boil. Dissolve some cornstarch in water until it is the consistency of heavy cream. Add a small amount of the cornstarch mixture to the boiling broth and stir until it thickens to the consistency of oil. Add more of the cornstarch mixture if necessary to achieve the correct consistency. Cool to room temperature before using.
  2. In a skillet, cook the bacon over medium-high heat until it crisps and the fat is released. Remove the bacon and set aside.
  3. Add the shallots and garlic to the bacon fat in the skillet and saute over medium heat until translucent. Add the brown sugar and stir until dissolved. Add the vinegar, thickened stock, and oil, and bring to a boil. Stir in the salt and pepper and keep warm.
  4. Place the spinach, mushrooms, and radicchio in a large bowl. Pour the warm dressing over the top and toss quickly until the spinach is slightly wilted.
  5. Divide the salad among 10 salad plates and garnish with the reserved bacon. Place 3 hush puppies on each plate. Serve immediately.
