Pate Brisee Crust Dough

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2.25 cup(s) cake flour sifted
  • 0.67 cup(s) butter cold, cut into small pieces
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) sugar
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) water 1-1/2 to 2 T, cold
  • 1 unit(s) egg yolks beaten

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour and butter. Rub the butter into the pastry flour with your fingers until it resembles coarse meal.
  2. In a small bowl, dissolve the salt and sugar in the water. Add the egg yolk and stir to combine. Gradually stir the egg yolk and water mixture into the flour mixture just until the dough pulls away from the side of the bowl.
  3. On a lightly floured board, form the dough into a ball, then flatten into a disk. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes or up to 2 days. Let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes before rolling out.
