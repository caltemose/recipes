Orange Salad with Olives and Cilantro

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup(s) orange juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste cayenne pepper
  • 0.33 cup(s) red onion(s) shaved
  • 6 unit(s) orange(s) mix of regular and blood orange, peeled and cut into 1/4-inch-thick slices
  • 0.33 cup(s) black olives sliced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) preserved lemon peel finely diced
  • 3 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped

Directions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together the orange juice, 1 tablespoon of the vinegar, and the oil. Season the dressing with salt and cayenne. Set aside.
  2. Place the onion in a bowl with the remaining 1 tablespoon vinegar and let stand until the color turns vivid red, about 10 minutes.
  3. Arrange the oranges, overlapping and alternating colors, on a serving platter or plates. Top with the olives and preserved lemon peel. Drizzle the dressing over the top and sprinkle with cilantro. Serve immediately.
