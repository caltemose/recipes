Tomato Salad with Warm Ricotta Cheese

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 pound(s) ricotta
  • 1 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.5 cup(s) olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 3 tablespoon(s) shallot(s) finely diced
  • 3 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 2 tablespoon(s) dry sherry vinegar
  • 1 sprig(s) basil
  • 1 cup(s) olive oil
  • 2 pound(s) tomato(es)
  • 1 tablespoon(s) basil chopped

Directions

  1. To make the ricotta cheese: Preheat the oven to 350 F. Mix the ricotta and salt together well. Put the mixture in a small baking dish or casserole. Drizzle with the olive oil and sprinkle with pepper. Bake until browned on top and bubbling around the edges, about 20 minutes. Let sit for about 10 minutes before serving.
  2. Meanwhile, combine the shallots, vinegars, salt, pepper, and basil sprig in a bowl. Let sit for 20 minutes.
  3. Remove and discard the basil sprig. Whisk in the olive oils. Adjust the flavor as necessary with additional vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cut any large tomatoes into halves or quarters and leave small ones whole. Add the tomatoes to the vinaigrette and toss to coat them evenly. Divide the tomatoes among 6 salad plates. Add a spoonful of the warm ricotta to each. Scatter the chopped basil over the top of the salads.
