Pistou Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 3 clove(s) garlic smashed and peeled
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 bunch basil leaves
  • 2 unit(s) tomato(es) small, peeled, seeded, and chopped
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 1 ounce(s) parmeggiano grated
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 5.5 ounce(s) ham hock or thick piece of smoked bacon
  • 7 ounce(s) cannellini beans fresh, shelled
  • 3.5 ounce(s) kidney beans or borlotti, fresh, shelled
  • 9 ounce(s) green beans sliced
  • 2 unit(s) potato floury, medium, diced
  • 3 unit(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded, and chopped
  • 4 unit(s) zucchini medium, chopped
  • 3.5 ounce(s) dry pasta macaroni

Directions

  1. To make the pistou, pound the garlic in a large mortar and pestle, then add a little salt and the basil and pound to a paste. Add the tomatoes and continue pounding and mixing until you have a thick sauce. Add pepper, the cheese, and the oil, mixing well, and adjust the seasoning.
  2. For the soup, put 2 quarts of cold water in a large stewing pot. Add the ham hock. Bring to a simmer, then partially cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes, skimming occasionally.
  3. Meanwhile, put the white and red beans in a saucepan, cover with plenty of cold water, and bring to a boil. Simmer for 10 minutes, drain, and refresh. Add all the vegetables to the stewing pot. Season lightly. Return to a simmer, then partially cover and simmer for 1 hour, skimming occasionally.
  4. Remove the ham hock and shred the meat. Remove half the ingredients out of the pan, mash with a fork, then return to the soup with the ham. Add the macaroni and cook until just tender. Stir in the pistou and serve.
