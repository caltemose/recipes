Beet and Orange Salad with Anise and Walnuts

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 pound(s) beet(s) washed well
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) anise seeds
  • 3 unit(s) cloves
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.12 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 2 tablespoon(s) dry sherry vinegar
  • 0.25 cup(s) olive oil
  • 0.25 cup(s) orange juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) red wine vinegar
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 2 cup(s) salad greens
  • 3 unit(s) orange(s) peeled and cut into 1/4-inch-thick slices
  • 0.5 cup(s) walnuts toasted and coarsely chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley coarsely chopped

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Place the beets on a large sheet of aluminum foil and sprinkle with the anise, cloves, salt, pepper, sherry vinegar, and 2 tablespoons of the oil. Wrap the foil around the beets. Place the beets in the oven and roast for about 45 minutes, depending on the size of the beets, until they are easily pierced with a knife. With a paring knife, remove the peels from the beets and cut them into bite-sized wedges. Set aside.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together the orange juice, red wine vinegar, and the remaining 2 tablespoons of the oil. Season the dressing with salt and pepper. Set aside.
  3. Arrange the greens on a serving platter or plates. Arrange the orange slices decoratively on top of the greens and top with the beets and walnuts. Drizzle the salad with the dressing and sprinkle with salt, pepper, and chopped parsley. Serve immediately.
