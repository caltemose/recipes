Carrot Salad with Radishes and Oranges

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon
  • 2 teaspoon(s) sugar
  • 1 teaspoon(s) orange flower water
  • 1 to taste salt
  • 1 to taste cayenne pepper
  • 3 unit(s) orange(s)
  • 8 unit(s) carrot(s) cut into large julienne
  • 9 unit(s) radish(es) sliced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley coarsely chopped

Directions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together the lemon juice, oil, cinnamon, sugar, and orange flower water. Season the dressing with salt and cayenne. Set aside.
  2. Cut the stem and blossom end from the oranges so that they sit flat on the cutting board. Using a sharp knife, slice away the peel and white pith from each orange, exposing the flesh below. Working over a bowl, cut between the membranes to free each orange segment, dropping the segments into the bowl as you work.
  3. Add the carrots and radishes to the bowl and toss the ingredients until well mixed. Taste and adjust the seasonings. Serve the salad immediately, garnished with the parsley.
