Grilled Beef Fillet with Chimichurri Sauce

CIA

Ingredients

  • 4 clove(s) garlic 4-6 cloves, sliced
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) salt plus more to taste
  • 0.33 cup(s) water
  • 0.33 cup(s) olive oil
  • 0.33 cup(s) red wine vinegar
  • 0.25 cup(s) red bell pepper(s) finely diced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) onion(s) minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) oregano minced
  • 1 unit(s) tomato(es) small, finely diced
  • 1 unit(s) jalapeño pepper(s) minced
  • 3 pound(s) beef tenderloin 3-4 pounds, trimmed
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) black pepper

Directions

  1. Place several bamboo skewers in a pan of water and let stand for at least 30 minutes. (This ensures that the skewers don't burn during grilling).
  2. In a food processor, combine the garlic, salt, water, oil, vinegar, bell pepper, onion, parsley, oregano, tomato, and jalapeño. Pulse the ingredients until a sauce-like consistency is reached. To make by hand, mince the garlic. Sprinkle the salt over the garlic and mash it into a paste with the flat side of a chef's knife. Transfer the garlic paste to a glass or stainless steel bowl and add the water, olive oil, vinegar, red pepper, onion, parsley, oregano, tomato, and jalapeño and mix well.
  3. Preheat the grill to high. Cut the beef into 1/2- by 1- to 2-1/2-inch pieces. Thread the beef onto the skewers and season the beef with salt and pepper. Grill the skewers until desired doneness, about 2 minutes per side for medium rare. Serve with the chimichurri sauce.
