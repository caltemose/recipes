Blackening Powder

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) black pepper
  • 1 unit(s) cayenne pepper
  • 1 unit(s) garlic powder
  • 1 unit(s) ground cumin
  • 1 unit(s) oregano dried
  • 1 unit(s) thyme dried
  • 2 unit(s) salt
  • 2 unit(s) paprika smoked

Directions

  1. Combine in a big jar, screw on the lid, and shake. Store in a cool place for up to 3 months.
