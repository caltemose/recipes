Gratin of Zucchini, Eggplant, and Chickpeas

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup(s) chickpeas dried
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 unit(s) yellow onion(s) small, quartered
  • 1 unit(s) bell pepper(s) seeded and cut into 1-inch squares
  • 3 unit(s) eggplant(s) Asian, about 3/4 lb total, cut crosswise into slices 1 inch thick
  • 3 unit(s) zucchini about 3/4 lb total, cut crosswise into slices 1 inch thick
  • 4 clove(s) garlic finely chopped
  • 2 cup(s) tomato(es) peeled and chopped (or 14 oz canned with the juices)
  • 1 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes optional
  • 0.25 cup(s) basil chopped, or 1 T dried
  • 1 teaspoon(s) thyme or 1/2 t dried
  • 0.5 cup(s) parmeggiano or other cheese such as herbed ricotta

Directions

  1. Pick over the chickpeas and discard any misshapen peas or stones. Rinse the chickpeas and rain. Place in a bowl, add plenty of water to cover, and let soak for 3 hours.
  2. Drain the chickpeas and place in a saucepan with water to cover by 2 inches. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat to low and simmer, uncovered, until tender, 45-60 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large frying pan over medium heat, warm the olive oil. Add the onions and bell pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft, about 10 minutes. Add the eggplants and zucchini and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until just lightly browned, about 10 minutes longer. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, for 1 minute.
  4. Increase the heat to high and add the tomatoes, vegetable stock, red pepper flakes, basil, thyme and chickpeas. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer, uncovered, for 30 minutes. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  5. Position a rack in the upper third of an oven and preheat to 375 F (190 C). Oil a shallow 9x13 baking dish.
  6. Pour the vegetable mixture into the prepared dish. Sprinkle the cheese evenly over the top. Bake until golden and bubbling around the edges, about 20 minutes.
  7. Let cool for 10 minutes. Spoon onto warmed plates and serve. Serve with bread to mop up the juices or polenta.
