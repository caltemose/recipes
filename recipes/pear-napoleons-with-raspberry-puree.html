Pear Napoleons with Raspberry Puree

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) butter at room temperature
  • 3 tablespoon(s) powdered sugar
  • 0.33 cup(s) honey
  • 0.25 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 1 unit(s) egg white(s)
  • 2 cup(s) sweet white wine
  • 0.5 unit(s) vanilla bean halved lengthwise
  • 1 cup(s) ricotta
  • 4 unit(s) pear(s) bartlett, peeled and cored
  • 2 tablespoon(s) honey
  • 0.75 cup(s) raspberry puree
  • 1 to taste powdered sugar for dusting

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or grease it.
  2. To make the honey crisps: In a medium bowl, cream the butter and powdered sugar together. Add the honey and blend thoroughly. Add the flour and stir until the mixture is smooth. Add the egg white and mix, scraping the bottom and sides of the bowl, until the batter is very smooth.
  3. Spread about 1 tablespoon of the batter on the prepared sheet to make a cookie about 3 inches in diameter. Leave 1 inch between the cookies. Bake for 3 to 4 minutes or until the edges just begin to brown. Using a flexible metal spatula, remove the honey crisps from the baking sheet and let cool on a wire rack.
  4. To make the filling: In a large saucepan, combine the wine and the vanilla bean. Bring to a simmer and add the pears. Poach until the pears are tender but still hold their shape. Let cool in the liquid. Drain and slice the pears.
  5. In a small bowl, mix the ricotta cheese and honey until thoroughly blended. Pool raspberry sauce on each plate. Spread the ricotta mixture on each of 6 crisps. Top with pear slices and a second crisp. Dust the tops with powdered sugar. Set each napoleon on a pool of sauce and serve at once.
