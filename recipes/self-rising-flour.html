Self-Rising Flour

Ingredients

  • 1 cup(s) all-purpose flour 100 grams
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt 1.13 grams
  • 1.25 teaspoon(s) baking powder 5.5 grams

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients to substitute for 1 cup of self-rising flour.
