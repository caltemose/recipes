Baked Apples

Me

Ingredients

  • 3 unit(s) apple(s)
  • 1 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) ground cinnamon
  • 2 pinch ground ginger
  • 0.5 unit(s) lemon(s)

Directions

  1. Slice and core apples. Cut into chunks.
  2. Melt the butter in a saucepan. Add the cinnamon and ginger and stir. Add the apple and saute until semi-soft, stirring occasionally. When cooked, squeeze 1/2 lemon into the pan and stir to coat the apples evenly.
  3. Store in a tightly sealed container in the fridge for up to a week.
