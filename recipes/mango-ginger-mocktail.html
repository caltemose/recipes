Mango Ginger Mocktail

The Indian Vegan Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) mango nectar
  • 1 unit(s) ginger tea made with fresh ginger
  • 2 unit(s) sparkling water
  • 1 unit(s) lime(s)
  • 1 sprig(s) mint for ganish

Directions

  1. To make ginger tea, slice ginger into thin rounds. (No need to peel the ginger.) Add ginger to a small saucepan of water and bring to a boil. Remove from heat and let steep for 8-10 minutes. Suggest to make the tea extra-strong, since you can always dilute it with water later.
  2. Mix mango nectar, ginger tea, and sparkling water in a high ball. Add squeeze of lime. Garnish and serve.
