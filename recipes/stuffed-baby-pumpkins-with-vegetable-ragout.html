Stuffed Baby Pumpkins with Vegetable Ragout

CIA

Ingredients

  • 6 unit(s) pumpkin miniature
  • 6 unit(s) asparagus peeled
  • 2 unit(s) carrot(s) peeled
  • 2 unit(s) red onion(s)
  • 1 unit(s) potato Idaho
  • 1 unit(s) zucchini
  • 0.5 cup(s) mushrooms white cultivated
  • 0.25 cup(s) heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon(s) dill chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped
  • 6 leave(s) basil chopped
  • 1 tablespoon(s) chive(s) chopped
  • 4 tablespoon(s) butter plus 3 T melted butter

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Cut lids from the pumpkins and scoop out the seeds. Place the pumpkins (including the lids) in a baking dish with 1/4 cup water. Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil.
  2. Cut the asparagus, carrots, red onions, potato, zucchini, and mushrooms into pieces. Place them on a baking sheet with a few spoonfuls of water. Cover the pan tightly with aluminum foil.
  3. Bake the pumpkin and vegetables until they are tender. The pumpkins will take about 20 minutes. The vegetables will take about 10-12 minutes.
  4. In a small saucepan, bring the cream to a boil. Add the herbs to the cream, reduce heat, and simmer until the cream is reduced by half. Add the butter to the cream and stir to melt. Add this mixture to the roasted vegetables. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Ladle the vegetable stew into the pumpkin shells. Replace the lids. Brush the pumpkins all over with a little melted butter to make them shine. Reheat in the oven for 15 minutes to be sure that all of the ingredients are very hot.
