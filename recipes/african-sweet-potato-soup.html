African Sweet Potato Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) chopped
  • 2 clove(s) garlic finely chopped
  • 2 teaspoon(s) ginger finely grated
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes crushed
  • 1 pound(s) sweet potato(es) cut into small chunks
  • 1 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) seeded and chopped
  • 1 can(s) tomato(es) 8 oz
  • 1 teaspoon(s) cumin seeds dry-roasted and crushed
  • 1 teaspoon(s) coriander seeds dry-roasted and crushed
  • 1 quart(s) vegetable stock hot
  • 2 tablespoon(s) peanut butter 2-3 T
  • 1 to taste hot sauce
  • 1 to taste olive oil chili oil, to serve
  • 1

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan, add the onion, and cook for 3 minutes. Stir in the garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes and cook for 1 minute, then stir in the sweet potatoes, pepper, tomatoes, and crushed spices and cook for 2 minutes. Pour in the stock, bring to a boil, then simmer for 12-15 minutes or until the sweet potatoes are tender.
  2. Remove from the heat and process in a blender until smooth. You may need to do this in batches. Pass the soup through a sieve to remove the pepper skin and then stir in the peanut butter, tasting to decide the desired amount. If you want to add more heat, add in some tabasco. Reheat gently, then drizzle a little chili oil over each bowlful to serve.
