Penny Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) leek(s) rinsed well
  • 11 ounce(s) potato
  • 9 ounce(s) carrot(s)
  • 6 ounce(s) sweet potato(es)
  • 1 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 12 cup(s) vegetable stock hot
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. Slice the vegetables into 1/8-inch rounds. Heat the oil and butter in a large saucepan, add the leeks, and cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, for 3-4 minutes or until soft. Add the potatoes, carrots and sweet potatoes and cook, stirring, for 1 minute.
  2. Pour in the stock, then bring to a boil, cover with a lid, and simmer for 20 minutes or until the vegetables are tender but not soft. Transfer about one-third of the vegetables to a blender with a little of the liquid and blend to a smooth puree, then return to the pan. Stir in the parsley, season to taste with salt and freshly ground black pepper, and serve with the vegetables in a little mound in the center.
