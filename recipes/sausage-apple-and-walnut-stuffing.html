Sausage, Apple and Walnut Stuffing

CIA

Ingredients

  • 6 tablespoon(s) butter
  • 0.5 cup(s) celery finely diced
  • 0.5 cup(s) onion(s) finely diced
  • 1 cup(s) sausage uncooked, crumbled
  • 6 cup(s) bread cubed
  • 1 cup(s) apple(s) peeled and diced
  • 0.5 cup(s) walnuts toasted and chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) chicken broth or as needed
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley minced

Directions

  1. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the celery and onion and saute for about 10 minutes, until the vegetables are tender. Add the sausage and saute until the sausage is crumbly and no pink remains. Stir in the bread cubes, tossing them with the other ingredients, and saute for about 1 minute. Remove the skillet from the heat.
  2. Transfer the sautéed mixture to a bowl. Add the apples and walnuts and toss the ingredients until mixed. If the stuffing needs additional moisture, add the chicken broth (it should be moist, but not wet). Add the parsley and season generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat the oven to 375 F. Place the stuffing in a baking pan and bake until it is hot throughout and the top is golden brown.
