Cranberry/Blueberry/Cherry-Pear Tart

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) pate brisee crust dough
  • 2 unit(s) egg(s) large
  • 0.5 cup(s) sugar
  • 0.12 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 0.5 cup(s) butter
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon(s) chocolate extract
  • 0.25 cup(s) all-purpose flour
  • 2 unit(s) pear(s) large Bartlett, peeled, cored, and cut into 1/2-inch slices
  • 1 cup(s) cranberries or blueberries or cherries

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. On a lightly floured board, roll the pate brisee into a circle 1/8 inch thick. Fit the pastry into a 10-inch tart pan with a removable bottom. Trim the pastry by running the rolling pin over the edges. Generously prick the bottom and sides of the dough with a fork.
  2. Line the pastry with parchment or waxed paper and fill it with pie weights or dried beans. Bake for 20 minutes or until the edges are set and dry. Remove the weights or beans and pepper and bake for 5 to 7 minutes or until golden brown. Let cool completely.
  3. In a medium bowl, combine the eggs, sugar, and salt. Beat on medium speed until the mixture is thick and pale in color.
  4. In a small, heavy saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat and continue cooking until it turns light brown. Remove from heat and add the lemon juice to stop the cooking process. Immediately strain the butter into the egg mixture and stir it until blended. Stir in the chocolate extract, flour, and cranberries.
  5. Line the pastry shell with the pears. Pour the batter over the fruit and bake for 50 to 60 minutes or until the fruit is tender and a crisp crust has formed on top. Serve warm or at room temperature.
