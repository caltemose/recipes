Cucumber Raita

Ingredients

  • 1 cup(s) yogurt nonfat, plain
  • 0.5 cup(s) cucumber(s) finely diced

Directions

  1. Pour the yogurt into a small-holed strainer set over a bowl. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and place in the refrigerator to drain overnight. Discard the liquid in the bowl and combine the yogurt and cucumber. Refrigerate until needed.
