Pate de Sardine

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 2 can(s) sardine(s) oil-packed, about 4 oz each
  • 2 ounce(s) butter at room temperature
  • 1 unit(s) shallot(s) large, chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 3 tablespoon(s) fresh herbs such as chives, parsley or dill, chopped

Directions

  1. Dump the sardines and their oil in the bowl of a food processor along with the butter, shallot, and lemon juice, and process until smooth. Add 2 tablespoons of the herbs and pulse 2 or 3 times.
  2. Pack the pate into a small ramekin. Smooth with a spatula, then top with the remaining 1 tablespoon herbs. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours to firm the texture. Tightly wrapped, the pate will keep for about 5 days.
