Ribollita

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) zuppa di verdure day-old, made up to the point just before adding the cavalo nero
  • 6 leave(s) cabbage cavolo nero or curly kale, tough stalks removed and leaves coarsely chopped
  • 6 slice(s) bread stale
  • 1 clove(s) garlic halved
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil 2-3 T, plus extra to serve
  • 0.5 unit(s) red onion(s) 1/2 to 1, very finely sliced
  • 4 tablespoon(s) parmeggiano grated

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 F. Bring the soup to a boil in a large pan, stir in the cavalo nero, and simmer for 10 minutes or until tender. Meanwhile, rub the stale bread with garlic.
  2. Oil a large gratin dish or similar ovenproof dish with olive oil, then spoon about a quarter of the soup into the dish, spreading it over the base. Now, lay half the slices of bread over the soup, then spoon over half of the remaining soup. Repeat these two layers one more time. Lay the red onion over the top, then scatter with the parmesan, if using. Drizzle over a little olive oil, then bake for 30 minutes. Serve sizzling hot, with extra olive oil on the table for those who want it.
