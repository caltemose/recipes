New England Corn Chowder

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil canola
  • 4 ounce(s) bacon finely diced
  • 1.5 cup(s) yellow onion(s) medium, finely diced
  • 3 stalk(s) celery finely diced
  • 2 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 2 teaspoon(s) thyme chopped
  • 2 cup(s) potato small dice
  • 2 cup(s) corn
  • 1 quart(s) chicken broth
  • 1 cup(s) half and half
  • 3 teaspoon(s) butter for garnish
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped, for garnish

Directions

  1. In a 3-quart saucepan, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the bacon and cook until the fat renders and it begins to turn brown and crisp.
  2. Add the onions and celery to the pan and saute over medium heat until the onions are translucent and the celery is softened.
  3. Add the bay leaves, thyme, potatoes, corn, and broth to the pan and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to low and simmer until the potatoes and corn are tender, about 20 minutes.
  4. Stir in the half-and-half, season with salt and pepper, and gently heat through.
  5. Ladle portions into heated soup plates or bowls garnished with 1 pat of butter for each portion, coarsely ground black pepper, and parsley.
