Goat Cheese-Stuffed Turkey Burgers with Roasted Red Pepper and Apricot Relish

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound(s) ground turkey
  • 6 tablespoon(s) bread crumbs toasted
  • 1.5 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) lemon zest
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) thyme chopped
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 6 tablespoon(s) goat cheese
  • 2 cup(s) roasted red pepper and apricot relish

Directions

  1. Light a fire in a charcoal or gas grill.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine the turkey meat, bread crumbs, lemon juice, lemon zest, thyme, salt, and pepper. Blend well. Divide the mixture into 12 equal portions and press them into 4-inch patties.
  3. Top each of the patties with 1 T goat cheese. Place a second patty on top and press down the edges to seal the two together. Place the patties on a baking sheet, cover, and refrigerate for 1 hour.
  4. Brush each burger with oil. Grill on an oiled grill rack over a hot fire for 5-6 minutes on each side, or until cooked through. Serve on an English muffin topped with relish.
