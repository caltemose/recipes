Beet and Apple Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 unit(s) onion(s) halved
  • 2 clove(s) garlic
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 12 ounce(s) beet(s) peeled and halved
  • 1 unit(s) potato halved
  • 4 unit(s) apple(s) peeled and cored
  • 1.5 quart(s) vegetable stock hot
  • 1 tablespoon(s) brown sugar
  • 1 unit(s) lemon(s) juiced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) fresh herbs finely chopped - parsley, chives, dill, and/or cilantro
  • 7 ounce(s) creme fraiche or sour cream, or yogurt

Directions

  1. <p class="p1" style="margin: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-feature-settings: normal; font-variation-settings: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Arial; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);"><span class="s1" style="font-kerning: none;">Briefly process the onions and garlic in a food processor. Heat the oil in a large pan over low heat, add the onion, garlic, and a pinch of salt, and cook gently, stirring once or twice, for 5 minutes or until soft. Meanwhile, process the beets, potato, and apples in the food processor.</span></p>
  2. <p class="p1" style="margin: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-feature-settings: normal; font-variation-settings: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Arial; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);"><span class="s1" style="font-kerning: none;">Add the beet mixture to the pan and stew gently for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Pour in the stock, bring to a boil, then cover with a lid and simmer gently for 45 minutes or until the beets are cooked through.</span></p>
  3. <p class="p1" style="margin: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-feature-settings: normal; font-variation-settings: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Arial; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);"><span class="s1" style="font-kerning: none;">Transfer the mixture to a blender and process until smooth. You may need to do this in batches. Season with sugar, lemon juice, and some salt and freshly ground black pepper.</span></p>
  4. <p class="p1" style="margin: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-feature-settings: normal; font-variation-settings: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Arial; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);"><span class="s1" style="font-kerning: none;">Stir the chopped herbs into the cream or yogurt, then ladle the soup into warm bowls and drop a big spoonful of the herbed cream into the middle of the soup.</span></p>
