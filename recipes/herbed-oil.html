Herbed Oil

Ingredients

  • 16 ounce(s) olive oil
  • 6 clove(s) large, crushed
  • 3 sprig(s) thyme
  • 2 sprig(s) rosemary
  • 10 leave(s) basil
  • 1 teaspoon(s) red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Combine the olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, basil, and red pepper flakes in a narrow saucepan over medium heat. As the heat builds, there will be considerable bubbling as the water cooks out of the garlic and herbs. When this dies down and the basil and garlic turn brown, kill the heat and let the mixture steep until cool.
  2. Strain the oil into a jar and toss the solids. Use immediately or cover tightly and freeze to keep the oil stable and safe. Will keep safely in the freezer for 2 months. If left at room temperature, the steeped oil can make spores that can cause botulism.
