Firecracker Shrimp

CIA

Ingredients

  • 1 pound(s) jumbo shrimp
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 2 teaspoon(s) lemon zest 1 lemon
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) cayenne pepper
  • 2 teaspoon(s) thyme minced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley minced
  • 5 ounce(s) pancetta thinly sliced
  • 1 unit(s) lemon(s) juiced

Directions

  1. Light a fire in a charcoal or gas grill.&nbsp; Shell the shrimp, leaving the tail on, and devein. In a medium bowl, combine the olive oil, lemon zest, cayenne, thyme, and all but 2 teaspoons of the parsley. Add the shrimp, stir to coat, and marinate for 1-2 hours. Wrap each shrimp in a slice of pancetta, overlapping the pancetta slightly with each turn to completely cover the shrimp. Thread the shrimp on skewers.
  2. Cook the shrimp over a hot fire for 2 minutes on each side, or until the pancetta begins to color and the shrimp are pink and opaque throughout.
  3. Remove the shrimp from the skewers. Toss with the lemon juice and reserved parsley. Serve at once.
