Cucumber-Yogurt Ranch Dressing

CIA

Ingredients

  • 0.33 cup(s) mayonnaise
  • 0.67 cup(s) yogurt
  • 0.25 unit(s) cucumber(s) peeled and pureed
  • 0.25 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) shallot(s) minced, rinsed in cold water
  • 1 teaspoon(s) Dijon mustard
  • 2 teaspoon(s) lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoon(s) white wine vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon(s) dill chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) tarragon chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) cilantro chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) parsley chopped

Directions

  1. In a medium bowl, combine all the ingredients and blend well. Season with salt and pepper. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. Serve now, or store for up to 2 days. Stir well and check the seasoning before serving.
