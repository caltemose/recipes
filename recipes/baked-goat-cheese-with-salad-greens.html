Baked Goat Cheese with Salad Greens

Williams-Sonoma

Ingredients

  • 3 unit(s) goat cheese small rounds, 2-1/2 inches diameter and 1 inch thick
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 unit(s) balsamic and red wine vinegar dressing
  • 1 clove(s) garlic minced
  • 1.5 cup(s) bread crumbs dried, finely ground
  • 1 bunch salad greens torn into bite-sized pieces

Directions

  1. Preheat an oven to 400 F (200 C).
  2. Cut each cheese round in half horizontally to make 6 rounds in all. Place on a plate and drizzle with olive oil, turning once to lightly coat both sides. Set aside.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the dressing, garlic, and salt and pepper to taste. Set aside.
  4. Spread the bread crumbs on a plate. Coat the rounds of goat cheese on both sides with the crumbs and then place them well spaced on a baking sheet. Bake until the cheese rounds are slightly bubbling around the edges, 4-6 minutes.
  5. Place the greens in a bowl and drizzle with the dressing. Toss well and divide the greens evenly among 6 salad plates. Place a hot cheese round in the center of each mound of greens. Serve immediately.
  6. Garnish the salad with slices of roasted bell peppers, black olives, or croutons. Accompany with slices of good crusty bread.
