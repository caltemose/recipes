Black-Eyed Pea Soup

Soup cookbook

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon(s) vegetable oil
  • 3 unit(s) leek(s) cleaned and sliced
  • 1 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) small, chopped
  • 1 unit(s) yellow bell peppers small, chopped
  • 3 clove(s) garlic
  • 2 inch ginger peeled and grated or coarsely chopped
  • 1 unit(s) red chiles small
  • 1 teaspoon(s) cumin seeds
  • 1 can(s) black-eyed peas 28 oz, drained, rinsed, and drained again
  • 1.75 cup(s) coconut milk
  • 2 cup(s) vegetable stock
  • 1 teaspoon(s) brown sugar
  • 1 to taste cilantro
  • 1 to taste lime(s) wedges, to serve

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a deep saucepan over medium heat, add the leeks and peppers, cover, and cook, stirring frequently, for 5-7 minutes or until soft.
  2. Meanwhile, put the garlic, ginger, chile, cumin, and 1 teaspoon salt in a blender with 4 tablespoons water and process until completely smooth. Add to the pan, using a little more water to rinse out every last bit. Cook, stirring, for 2-3 minutes or until most of the liquid has evaporated.
  3. Add the peas, coconut milk, stock, sugar, and a good grinding of black pepper and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat to a simmer and cook gently for 30 minutes, stirring frequently. Taste for seasoning, then serve each bowlful garnished with fresh cilantro and a lime wedge.
