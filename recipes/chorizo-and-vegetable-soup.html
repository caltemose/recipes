Chorizo and Vegetable Soup

CIA

Ingredients

  • 2.5 ounce(s) chorizo casings removed, diced
  • 1.5 teaspoon(s) olive oil
  • 0.25 cup(s) onion(s) diced
  • 2 tablespoon(s) celery diced
  • 1 teaspoon(s) garlic minced
  • 0.75 teaspoon(s) cumin seeds
  • 2.5 cup(s) chicken broth
  • 2 cup(s) tomato(es) peeled, seeded and diced
  • 1 unit(s) potato medium, peeled and diced
  • 2 unit(s) red bell pepper(s) small, diced
  • 1 tablespoon(s) tomato paste
  • 0.5 unit(s) bay leaf
  • 1 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) oregano dried
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 cup(s) corn
  • 0.25 teaspoon(s) black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon(s) cilantro chopped

Directions

  1. Heat a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the chorizo and saute until browned, about 5 to 10 minutes. Transfer the chorizo to paper towels to drain, and discard the fat from the pan.
  2. Add the oil to the pan over medium heat. Add the onion, celery, garlic, and cumin seeds and saute until the onion is translucent, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the chorizo, broth, tomatoes, potato, peppers, tomato paste, bay leaf, half of the parsley, oregano, and salt and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to low and simmer until the potatoes are tender, about 20 minutes. Discard the bay leaf. Stir in the corn and black pepper and simmer until heated through.
  4. Ladle the soup into warm soup bowls and garnish with the cilantro and parsley.
