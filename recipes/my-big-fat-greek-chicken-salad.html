My Big Fat Greek Chicken Salad

Alton Brown

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup(s) greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoon(s) lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoon(s) parsley chopped
  • 0.5 teaspoon(s) salt
  • 1 to taste black pepper
  • 1 pound(s) chicken cooked, torn and roughly chopped
  • 1 unit(s) cucumber(s) small, peeled, seeded and chopped
  • 1 unit(s) tomato(es) Roma, chopped
  • 0.5 unit(s) red onion(s) small, finely chopped
  • 0.5 cup(s) feta cheese crumbled
  • 0.5 cup(s) kalamata olive(s) roughly chopped

Directions

  1. &lt;p class="p1" style="mWhisk the yogurt, lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper to taste together in a medium bowl. Add the chicken, cucumber, tomato, red onion, feta, and olives and stir.
  2. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving nestled down in a warm pita. Will keep, covered, in the fridge for 3 days.
